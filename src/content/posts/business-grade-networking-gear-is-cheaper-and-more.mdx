## The Details

Buy a 2 or 3 pack, put the nodes around your home, follow the setup wizard, and you should have better coverage. The problem is that a decent mesh system can become surprisingly expensive, especially when you start looking at higher-end models.

After using both consumer mesh systems and business-oriented networking equipment, I have found that dedicated APs can make more sense for many homes. They require a little more planning, but they can provide better performance, more configuration options, and a network that is easier to expand without buying another proprietary mesh node.
