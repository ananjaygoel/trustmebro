## The Details

When WandaVision premiered in 2021, ushering in Marvels Disney+ era, no one anticipated that the Elizabeth Olsen-led series would expand into a narrative saga of its own within the MCU. Five years and one Agatha All Along later, VisionQuest ends this trilogy by—what else—setting up the future.

But before a post-credits scene which sees Wanda Maximoffs resurrection, Forever once again pits the synthezoid Vision (Paul Bettany) against his old frenemy/pseudo-dad, Ultron (James Spader). Theres also the reveal of The Clients identity, a proper reunion between brothers Tommy (Ruaridh Mollica) and Billy (Joe Locke), and a return to WandaVision s sitcom riffs.
