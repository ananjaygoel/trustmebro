## What's Happening

So get this: Since his arrival in Boulder, Deion Sanders made Colorado relevant.

Maintaining relevancy is what comes next, though. Colorado athletic director Fernando Lovo just decided to go for a solo stroll around campus. (shocking, we know)

He stopped inside the University Memorial Center student building and noticed a massive banner that serves as a tribute.

## The Details

As another college football season begins, it also marks the passage of time. The banner lists the details of “The Prime Effect” — what Deion Sanders’ arrival as Colorado coach in December 2022 has done for the university, football program and city of Boulder.

Lovo described the various statistics plastered on the banner, including the hundreds of millions of dollars of economic impact , the explosion of merchandise and season-ticket sales, the number of CU applicants surging , and the once-gaudy TV viewership numbers.
