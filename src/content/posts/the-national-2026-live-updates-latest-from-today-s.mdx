## The Details

For the uninitiated, the National is an annual convention now in its 46th year. What began with a few hobbyists setting up folding tables at a Marriott near a Los Angeles airport is now billed the world’s largest gathering of sports collectors, dealers, athletes, breakers and media members.

More than 100,000 people attended last year’s event. Our team on the show floor will guide you through Thursday with videos, photos, regular updates and conversations with attendees.
