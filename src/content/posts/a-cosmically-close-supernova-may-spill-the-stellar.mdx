## Why This Matters

Soon after scientists spotted the explosion on November 2, they determined it hadn’t yet peaked in brightness, giving scientists an early glimpse at the event. Even more thrilling, within a day of the explosion’s discovery, astronomers had classified it as a type Ia supernova. These particular outbursts are rare, but they’re astronomical superstars: Scientists use their trademark explosion pattern as a “standard candle” to measure distance into the universe — most notably to discover that the universe’s expansion is accelerating .
