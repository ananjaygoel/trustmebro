## What's Happening

Listen up: Apple has approached publishers to establish content deals that would give Siri AI access to current news and information, reports The Wall Street Journal.

Apple is proposing multiyear agreements where publishers would receive payment when their content is used, which is different from standard AI deals. Typical deals between AI companies and news organizations have set fees for broad content access. (plot twist fr)

Apple has discussed a nine-figure budget for the payments.

Siri AI is a major departure from the prior version of Siri , and Apple has an incentive to ensure accurate news headlines. Apple wrapped up up removing news summaries for more than a year while it made improvements.
