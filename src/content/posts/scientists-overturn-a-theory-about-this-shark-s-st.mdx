## What's Happening

Okay so Scientists have overturned a leading explanation for why male and female bonnethead sharks have differently shaped heads.

Instead of males developing a pointed snout at maturity, both sexes actually begin with more pointed heads that become rounder with age, particularly in females. Science News Bonnethead sharks are famous for their unusual shovel-shaped heads, but scientists may have been wrong about how those shapes develop. (shocking, we know)
