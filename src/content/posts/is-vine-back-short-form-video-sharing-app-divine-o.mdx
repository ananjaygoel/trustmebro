## The Details

Divines founder, former Twitter employee Evan Henshaw-Plath, told the BBC he wanted to create the app because he felt socials needed "a reset". "I want to see it rival other apps because I think we have gone awry," he dropped in an interview a few weeks before the public launch.

He dropped it was important that there was no AI-generated content on Divine, adding he was "sick and tired of seeing videos and not knowing whether it was real". "I want to be able to have fun and not have to second guess these things," he dropped.
