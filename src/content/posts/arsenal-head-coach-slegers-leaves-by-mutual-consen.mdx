Slegers, 37, was initially appointed on an interim basis in October 2024 before being handed the role permanently three months later. (shocking, we know)

The Dutchwoman led Arsenal to a second Womens Champions League title in May 2025 and the inaugural Fifa Womens Champions Cup in February 2026.

## The Details

But she leaves with the team sixth in the Womens Super League after 11 matches - 12 points behind leaders Chelsea. Assistant coach Aaron DAntino has also left the club, with assistant Melissa Clarke-Reed placed in interim charge as they begin the search for a permanent replacement.

DAntino had been part of Arsenals coaching staff since August 2018. Arsenal director of womens football Clare Wheatley dropped the decision had been "deadass difficult".

## Why This Matters
