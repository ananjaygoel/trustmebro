## The Details

Vision's unique existential burden transforms him into the ultimate observer of the Avengers, always balancing his analytical computer mind with a fragile and clean soul that makes his capacity for self-sacrifice especially moving compared to the more conventional power fantasies of other heroes. Vision's synthezoid body is something of an evolutionary canvas, as his physical shell can undergo radical transformations and rebirths that organic beings can't experience.

Composed of synthetic flesh and cybernetic circuitry, and powered by a solar gem embedded in his forehead, Vision's body can be entirely disassembled, reprogrammed, and reassembled. This mechanical plasticity led to his iconic White Vision incarnation during Marvel Comics' Vision Quest storyline , where he was stripped of his human brainwave patterns and resurrected as an emotionless chalk-white weapon.
