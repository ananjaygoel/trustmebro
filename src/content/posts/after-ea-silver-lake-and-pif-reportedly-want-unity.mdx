## What's Happening

So get this: Silver Lake and PIF, who took EA private, are reportedly eyeing Unity at around $25bn.

The engine behind thousands of games could be next. Unity software has held talks to be acquired by a consortium that includes private equity firm Silver Lake and Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund, according to Reuters. (shocking, we know)

The group discussed a valuation of around $25 billion, whilst Unity’s market cap sits at roughly $21.
