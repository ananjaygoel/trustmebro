## The Details

Instead, we got a long wait for whatever Activision is making. On October 6, reliable Halo insider and reporter Rebs Gaming d a new video report that claims theyve heard from their sources that a team of around 30 people who werent included in the recent Xbox layoffs is still working on Halo: Campaign Evolved .

This small team is reportedly being led boss Pierre Hintze, and their goal is to help out with the upcoming Halo Fest event while also developing one last free content update for the just dropped remake. According to Rebs, this isnt some tiny update comprising a few bug fixes and quality-of-life changes, but instead, the current plan is to add Firefight and a Theater Mode to Campaign Evolved.

## Why This Matters

Firefight was a popular horde mode featured in past games like Halo: Reach, and its addition to Campaign Evolved would be awesome, though Rebs does caution that plans could change depending on what the small team can get done. And it sounds like once the team is done with Halo Fest in December and the update, the studio will be completely shut down.
