The most stylish way to wear them this summer is with a kitten heel, according to fashion expert and trend forecaster Angela Baidoo.

## The Details

"Its not a high heel and its fr kind of easy to just slip in and slip out of," she says. But, heel or not, the flip-flop has earned itself a rough rap for lack of support and damaging our feet.

Why your summer shoe could be rough for you "for arch support and joint support, its not the best style, but people are investing a bit more, getting their flip-flops customised, because its something that is just so easy to wear," Baidoo says. Podiatrist Dr Helen Branthwaite, who is also chief clinical adviser at the Royal College of Podiatry, says its "not accurate" to say flip-flops are rough.

## Why This Matters

The problem comes if you go straight from wearing a structured shoe to wearing flip-flops a lot, she explains. It means your foot is under more strain than normal, which makes you more prone to injury. "The foot does start to change shape and tends to splay a little bit over time," says Branthwaite.

Medical professionals are taking note of this development.
