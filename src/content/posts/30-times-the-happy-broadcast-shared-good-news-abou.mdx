## The Details

Every post pairs a real story with one of Gatti’s signature drawings: simple, round-faced characters and cheerful creatures set against bold, bright backgrounds. The Instagram page now has over 807,000 followers and more than 1,500 posts.

The idea has also grown into a TikTok account, a 2020 book and WHOLE, a mental wellness app the artist runs with his business partner Keith Bonnici. Animal rights were among the topics Gatti wanted to see covered from the start, and his book even features a cuddle club that pairs senior dogs with elderly people.
