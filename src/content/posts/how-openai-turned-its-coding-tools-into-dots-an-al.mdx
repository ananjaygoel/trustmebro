## What's Happening

Okay so Alexander Embiricos, the OpenAI executive responsible for dots, never wants to have a meeting with someone else's AI agent.

The always-on AI personal assistant, which keeps working in the background even when you aren’t asking it to, is this years answer to the question of how people will actually use deep learning models. The startup Instinct became an overnight sensation, and Meta put the full force of its marketing behind its assistant, Muse. (and honestly, same)

OpenAI wasnt going to be left out.

## Why This Matters

The company dropped dots , its assistant offering, at its developer day in late September. Alexander Embiricos, who leads OpenAIs enterprise product team, joined us at TechCrunch Disrupt this month to explain where the product came from—starting with the question of why so many companies seem to be turning to cutesy mascots as avatars for their trillion-parameter matrix multiplication machines.

This is part of the broader shift happening across the tech industry right now.
