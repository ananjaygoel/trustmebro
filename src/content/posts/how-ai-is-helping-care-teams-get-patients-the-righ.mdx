To understand one patient’s full story, a clinician would need to read through years of charts .

## Why This Matters

Dyania Health , a New Jersey-based clinical AI company, built Synapsis AI to do just that. Synapsis AI is a clinical reasoning engine: It works across every note, report and lab result in a patients record, connecting findings that may be years apart to answer clinical questions the way a specialist reviewing the chart would. The output is a set of clinical signals, each traceable to its source in the record, that tells care teams which patients need attention and why.
