## What's Happening

So get this: Is Apple hoping to get into the AI-generated podcast business?

Apple spilled in a regulatory filing that it has reached an agreement to bring on team members and technology from personalized audio startup Huxe, in what’s commonly known as a reverse acqui-hire deal. (plot twist fr)

As first reported in MacRumors , Apple disclosed to the European Commission that it has agreed to make employment offers to “certain employees of Huxe AI,” and to “receive a non-exclusive license to Huxe’s intellectual property rights.
