## The Details

I have no idea about animation, Weisman dropped, but there are no current plans for live-action. In 2023, Disney just dropped a live-action reboot of the 90s cartoon about four stone statues trying to find love and themselves in the Big Apple, with Goliath (voiced ) serving as the series Carrie Bradshaw.

(For the record: Ed Asners Hudson was obviously the Samantha. ) The idea was to get Conjuring spin-off writer Gary Dauberman and James Wans Atomic Monster to develop a fresh take on the series for Disney+.
