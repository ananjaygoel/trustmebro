## Why This Matters

Credit: Anadolu / Contributor for Getty Tesla threw a flashy party on Thursday to announce the start of its autonomous Cybercab service in Austin , but some of the wind was taken out of the company's sails when the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration just dropped an investigation into the Cybercabs almost ASAP after the launch. Tesla s Cybercabs are gold-covered robotaxis that seat two riders; the Cabs offer a luxe, roomy experience thanks to butterfly doors, a large Grok -enabled video screen, and the absence of a steering wheel and gas and brake pedals. The omission of the pedals and wheel is exactly why the NHTSA is looking into the Cybercabs, as the agency requires autonomous vehicles to have those back-up controls.

This could have major implications for how we use technology going forward.
