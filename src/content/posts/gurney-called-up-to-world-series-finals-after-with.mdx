## What's Happening

So get this: Daryl Gurney has been called up to play at the World Series of Darts Finals in Amsterdam after visa issues led to the withdrawal of Philippines players Lourence Ilagan and Alexis Toylo from the September 17-20 event.

Northern Ireland's Gurney and the Netherlands' Maik Kuivenhoven were the highest-ranked players on the reserve list and have replaced Ilagan and Toylo respectively after the pair did not receive a decision on their Schengen visa applications before having to travel to China this week for the final two events of the PDC Asian Tour season.
