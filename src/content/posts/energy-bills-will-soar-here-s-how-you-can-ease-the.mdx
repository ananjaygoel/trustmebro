## The Details

Millions of people have cut back on their energy consumption during years of relatively high prices. With the unit price of gas, in particular, set to soar, the experts say you should take a fresh look at your energy use and renew good habits.

Research foundation Nesta suggests: turning down the flow temperature on a combi boiler - usually indicated by a radiator icon, to make it operate more efficiently turning off the pre-heat facility on some combi boilers, which would have kept some water heated up when not in use turning down the thermostat one degree, although 18C is the recommended minimum closing all curtains at night lowering radiator valves outside of the living room turning off the heating before April These are small changes but - alongside washing clothes at 30C, blocking draughts and batch cooking - they can reduce energy use and bills.
