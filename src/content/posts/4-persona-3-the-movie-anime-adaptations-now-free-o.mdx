## What's Happening

Listen up: All four Persona 3 the Movie anime adaptations of the game's story are now temporarily free to stream online.

Anime News 4 Persona 3 the Movie Anime Adaptations Now Free on YouTube By Jenni Lada Image via Aniplex and Atlus We can now catch up on the four Persona 3 the Movie anime adaptations for free thanks to Aniplex and Atlus sharing them on YouTube. This is part of the 30th anniversary celebration of the series. (shocking, we know)

Normally, it can cost about $14.

## The Details

99 to rent each of these, depending on the film. This is a limited time offer, though the companies didn’t note how long we can stream them without paying.

Recommended Videos Each of these movies is about an hour and a half long. All of them are available with Japanese voice acting and English subtitles.

## Why This Matters

All of them together cover the events of the game , with each part covering a different segment of the story. Note that this is a retelling that focuses on the original tale with Makoto Yuki as the protagonist and doesn’t cover any of the Persona 3 Portable additional content. Here’s the full list of all four parts of the Persona 3 anime adaptation: Part 1 : Spring of Birth Part 2 : Midsummer Knight’s Dream Part 3: Falling Down Part 4: Winter of Rebirth And you can get kicked off with Persona 3 the Movie 1: Spring of Birth below.
