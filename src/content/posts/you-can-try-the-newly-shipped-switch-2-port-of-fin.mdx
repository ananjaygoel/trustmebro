Perhaps the best news is that the game arrives on Nintendo's hybrid console with a free trial, which includes quite a bit of content: the base game and three pretty sizeable expansions in Heavensward , Stormblood and Shadowbringers .

You can download this now, and play today, at absolutely no cost. You will, but, have to set up both a Nintendo Switch Online account and a Square Enix account to access the game, though a subscription to Nintendo Switch Online is not required to play.

## Why This Matters

You will be capped at level 80 in the free trial, and you don't get to use the full suite of multiplayer features, engage in PvP, or try out the Ultimate Raids. Still, as a way of seeing if this critically- and commercially-successful MMORPG is for you, it's a good way in.
