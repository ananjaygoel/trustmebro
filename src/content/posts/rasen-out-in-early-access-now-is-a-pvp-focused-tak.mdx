## The Bottom Line

And best of all, unusually for a 3D arena fighter, it has rollback netcode. For those unfamiliar, and to try and explain it simply, this is essentially a bit of tech you'll find in predominantly 2D fighting games that predicts every possible input you could make in a given moment, helping to reduce lag time and allow for tighter matches (and less rage quitting).
