## The Details

The chicks have had rings placed on their legs so that they can be recorded in any other areas they turn up in after leaving the nest. People are being encouraged to report any sightings of barn owls across Northern Ireland, particularly in the west, to help fill in gaps in the records and even help protect important breeding areas.
