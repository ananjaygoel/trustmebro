## Why This Matters

In a secret first straw poll of the UN security council, the five permanent and 10 temporary members were asked to say whether they backed, objected to or were neutral about each of seven candidates. Grynspan secured 10 “encourage” votes, four “no opinion” votes and one “discourage” vote. The identity of the discourage vote is not known, but the fact that she had only one objection places her in a strong opening position.
