At least 1,000 flights in or out of the country have been cancelled, according to tracking site FlightRadar24. (it feels like chaos)

EasyJet has made 200 cancellations and British Airways has made more than 100.

## The Details

Nats dropped on Tuesday evening that its flight systems were starting to recover. With few aircraft able to take off, it is understood landings are being restricted, leading to some flights being prevented from departing from overseas airports.

The fault was widespread across the UK, and beyond, with airports in Scotland and Ireland also reporting disruption. Europes busiest airline, Ryanair, earlier reported that more than 65,000 passengers had been delayed eight hours At Heathrow, one of the busiest airports in Europe, departures were initially paused around lunchtime.

## Why This Matters

Traffic then resumed, before facing further disruption in the afternoon. It is understood that the airport ran out of space for incoming flights during the afternoon because planes had not been departing. Thirteen Heathrow-bound flights were diverted to other airports in the UK and Europe.
