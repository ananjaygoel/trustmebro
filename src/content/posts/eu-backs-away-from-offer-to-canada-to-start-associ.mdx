## The Details

27 EDT First Wed 28 Oct 2026 17. 06 EDT Prefer the Guardian on Google The EU has backed away from its offer to Canada to start talks on becoming an “associate member” after disagreements between members states just as an EU-Canada summit was set to begin in Montreal.

The issue was triggered after the European Commission president, Ursula von der Leyen , surprised nearly everyone last month’s state of the union address that the EU would consider “associate membership” for Canada. The declaration had been hailed as a landmark in EU history, offering a non-EU country such close ties to the bloc, which has not allowed any state membership since Croatia joined in 2013.
