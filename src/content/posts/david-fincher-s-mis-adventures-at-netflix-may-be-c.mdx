## The Details

Bloomberg reports Fincher has opted not to renew his contract with Netflix, which dropped his last two movies, Mank and The Killer , as well as the upcoming Once Upon A Time… In Hollywood sequel, The Further Mis-Adventures Of Cliff Booth . The Fincher-Netflix deal expires next year, so the two parties could still come to terms.

Fincher signed with the streamer in 2020, a year when signing an exclusive with a streamer offering to make your high-priced passion projects made a whole lot of business sense. But, his work since signing has not had the healthy post-theatrical life that, say, Zodiac or Fight Club enjoyed.
