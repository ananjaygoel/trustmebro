52 EST Two skulls, including that of a man believed to have taken part in Brazil’s greatest slave revolt but whose remains were taken to the United States and used in racist eugenics studies at Harvard University , were repatriated on Wednesday. The skulls are believed to have belonged to Africans or Afro-Brazilians in the country that received more enslaved Africans than any other during the transatlantic slave trade.

## Why This Matters

Each was brought back in a padded box inside a reinforced suitcase, carried on flights after a four-year campaign to recover the remains from Harvard’s Peabody Museum of Archaeology and Ethnology in the US. Brazilian researchers from local universities will now try to learn more about who the two individuals were.
