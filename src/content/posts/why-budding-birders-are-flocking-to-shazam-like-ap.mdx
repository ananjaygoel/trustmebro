## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, Birdwatchers tell the BBC how apps have changed their lifelong hobby - and attracted new birders.

Why budding birders are flocking to Shazam-like apps to identify avians Image source, Megan Szostak Image caption, Megan has been birding since childhood, but Merlin Bird ID has become part of her toolkit By Liv McMahon Technology reporter Published 1 hour ago Megan Szostak, 26, was always a "bird kid". The urge to watch and appreciate the world around her - and avians flitting in and out of it - has been ingrained in her since childhood, when she was given her grandfathers binoculars after he died. (and honestly, same)

Armed with this and a field guide in her home of San Francisco, she set about identifying birds from an early age.

## The Details

But in recent years, her birding toolbelt has expanded to include something far more modern - an app. "I had friends who probably wouldnt describe themselves as birders, but more just curious about nature and the world around them, recommend it to me," Megan dropped.

She is one of many millions of people who have downloaded Merlin Bird ID, which claims to be able to identify almost any bird , song or call. "I think that its been a fr solid tool that Ive integrated into my birding routine," she dropped.

## Why This Matters

And whether down to heatwaves sending more people outdoors, or younger people delving into birding, these apps are booming in popularity. Chief among them is Merlin Bird ID, which was developed s Lab of Ornithology. It has been downloaded 12 million times in 2026 alone, according to director Jessie Barry - who says it has had 42 million downloads overall.

This is part of the broader shift happening across the tech industry right now.

## The Bottom Line

She says the app has come a long way since it first shipped in the US in 2014, with ML systems which could identify 285 common bird species from photos. A real feather in its cap at the time, but a decade or so on, and it can now identify 11,000 species of birds - around as many as exist in the world.
