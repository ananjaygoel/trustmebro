## The Details

Much like Critical Role ’s arrival inspired many nerds to take a new interest in tabletop role-playing games, Baldur’s Gate 3 had a similar effect. Players wanted to learn more about the world their favorite companions came from, and D&D publisher Wizards of the Coast was ready with supplementary material, from miniatures and character sheets to crossover appearances in games like Idle Champions of the Forgotten Realms and Magic: The Gathering .

Wizards of the Coast also just dropped four tie-in books from Penguin Random House, including an Astarion Acunin prequel novel (out now) from award-winning Nettle & Bone author T. Kingfisher (aka, Ursula Vernon).

## Why This Matters

While all the B aldur’s Gate 3 companions are loved, the witty elf-vampire Astarion in particular has inspired a fervent, devoted fanbase, with many empathizing with the abuse he suffered at the hands of his cruel vampire master, Cazador. Kingfisher has proven more than capable of handling dark, grim topics in books like The Twisted Ones , so it’s little wonder Wizards chose her to handle Astarion. With input from Astarion’s writer , Stephen Rooney, and voice actor, Neil Newbon, Kingfisher delivers an emotional whirlwind of a novel in Baldur's Gate 3: Astarion .

Gaming fans have strong opinions about moves like this, and for good reason.
