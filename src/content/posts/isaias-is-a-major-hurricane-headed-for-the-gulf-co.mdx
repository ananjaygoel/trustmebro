## The Details

After the longest wait on record in the satellite era, this years Atlantic hurricane season has its first hurricane: Hurricane Isaias. Isaias is now a powerful, major Category 3 hurricane with sustained winds up to 120 miles per hour (193 kph).

CDT Friday, October 9, is 245 miles (390 km) south of Pensacola, Florida. Its taking aim at the Gulf Coast with landfall expected Friday night near the Alabama and Florida state line.

## Why This Matters

Strong wind shear could weaken the storm before it makes landfall, but dangerous and life-threatening impacts are still expected in the form of up to 7 feet (2 meters) of storm surge and heavy rain of more than 6 inches (15 cm) causing flash flooding. Plus, theres the risk of hurricane-force winds and tornadoes on the eastern side of the storm. Isaias formed as a tropical storm in the southwestern Gulf on Wednesday morning, October 7.
