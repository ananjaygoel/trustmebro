## The Details

This fall, WIRED made a special issue devoted to gender and power . On October 8, for the live-audience recording of the Uncanny Valley podcast in San Francisco, WIRED dug further into the fascinating and nuanced state of affairs.

First up: a special conversation between Paulina Borsook , tech journalist and former WIRED writer, and WIRED global editorial director Katie Drummond . Borsook, whose prescient 2000 book Cyberselfish is being rereleased this fall, has long been a skeptic of the tech world—which might have been to her detriment during the growth mindset of the past few decades.
