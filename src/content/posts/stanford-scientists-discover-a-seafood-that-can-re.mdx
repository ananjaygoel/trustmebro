## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, A substance found in edible sea squirts appeared to reverse several signs of aging in older mice.

Plasmalogen supplements improved memory and learning, strengthened connections between brain cells, reduced inflammation, and even helped the mice grow thicker, darker hair. (yes, really)

Researchers believe the compounds may encourage brain regeneration and protect aging synapses.
