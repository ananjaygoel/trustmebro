## Why This Matters

When the Coach is enabled, it updates the main screen of the app with a few paragraphs of text several times a day. (You ll also get a notification with each of these updates). The Coach will tell you how well you slept, congratulate you on a hard workout, or suggest workouts for the day given how recovered you seem to be.

This is part of the broader shift happening across the tech industry right now.

## Key Takeaways

- One user, returning to their normal routine after surgery, posted to Reddit that the Coach keeps trying to talk them out of doing workouts.
