## The Details

Follow World of Warcraft: Forever Prioritise us on Google As World of Warcraft: Forever players dived into the game, they quickly found out a specific weapon enchant that took area of effect spells to a whole other level. The enchantment in question was Revelation - a new addition to the game included as part of the new trade good reputations.

What it does is simple: when you fail to deal a critical strike with a spell, the next spell hit is guaranteed to do so. What made this particularly powerful is that the buff also worked with continuous area of effect spells, meaning an early instance of the spell not crit-ing would result in the remaining damage hits all dealing critical damage.
