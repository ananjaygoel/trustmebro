## Key Takeaways

- To complete a Map Kit, you need to first locate the large tower covered in dishes (this will be marked on your map whenever you enter a new region).
- When you locate the tower, there will be three smaller satellites near will need to activate.
- These satellites will have blinking red lights, and interacting with one will cause it to point towards the tower.
