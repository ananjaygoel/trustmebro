The rare celestial event will be the most dramatic eclipse visible from the UK since 1999, with Cornwall, Devon, Somerset and Bristol among the best places in the UK to watch it.

## The Details

People in Bristol, Somerset, Gloucestershire and Wiltshire are expected to see around 90% of the Sun obscured, while Cornwall and the Isles of Scilly should get some of the best views anywhere in the UK with 95%. While the UK will miss out on a total eclipse, astronomers say this will be the most wild solar eclipse visible from Britain since the much-remembered event of August 1999.
