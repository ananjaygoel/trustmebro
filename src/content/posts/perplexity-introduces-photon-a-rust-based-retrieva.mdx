## What's Happening

Breaking it down: Perplexity has dropped Photon, an in-house retrieval and ranking engine written in Rust.

It replaces an open-source engine Perplexity had forked for its AI-native search stack. Photon now handles retrieval and ranking for all production traffic. (it feels like chaos)

It also powers a new Fast Search mode in the Perplexity Search API.

## Why This Matters

Why Perplexity Replaced its Old Engine The old engine hit 3 limits as the index grew: Tail latency: Production p99 sat near 800 ms. The dataset exceeded RAM, so mlock was not an option. Cold reads triggered major page faults that stalled queries.

## Key Takeaways

- Merge spikes: During disk index fusion, p99 climbed to about 1.
- Slow recovery: Deploying and syncing an extra cluster could take more than a week.
- Recovery also raised the of partial responses.
- Perplexity team concluded that building from scratch was simpler and cheaper than maintaining its fork.
