## Key Takeaways

- The same tasks are reused every round, so the loop can memorize them.
- The RRSI research names 3 failure modes: benchmark-specific fitting, noise chasing and complexity accumulation.
- Each one widens the gap between evolve-set scores and real transfer.
- How RRSI Works RRSI keeps every harness component editable.
