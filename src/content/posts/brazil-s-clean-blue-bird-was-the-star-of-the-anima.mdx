## The Details

Leaving behind the industrial city of Petrolina in northeast Brazil, the eight-vehicle convoy, escorted Police SUVs, crossed the São Francisco River and, for two hours, traversed the Caatinga, a stark landscape of thorn scrub, cactuses and drought-stricken ranch land. At dawn, the procession turned off the highway and jolted down a dirt road to a fenced compound.

The police presented a judicial order. Then the vehicles drove up to a cluster of buildings housing 103 birds that rank among the rarest animals on Earth: Spix’s macaws, luminous blue parrots that vanished from the wild a quarter-century ago.
