## Why This Matters

I would play it while commuting on the train or during lunch. I’d go through every cup, do every challenge, and slowly build up my characters and karts until they reached their maximum potential (Level 8 with variable score depending on their rank). It was a big part of my life through the COVID days, when moving house and city, and even through career changes.
