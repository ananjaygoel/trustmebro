NGC 4449: Small Galaxy, Big Impact Explanation: Don’t judge a galaxy . Our home galaxy, the Milky Way , is about 100,000 light years across.

This huge expanse of space contains hundreds of billions of stars! The smallest galaxies, called dwarf galaxies , are about 1/10th the size of the Milky Way and only contain a few billion stars.

## Why This Matters

They are also the most common type of galaxy in the whole universe. Today’s image shows one such dwarf galaxy, NGC 4449 , captured by the Hubble Space Telescope . NGC 4449 and similar dwarf galaxies are missing more oxygen than models predict.
