## The Details

The deployment comes as the two neighbours rebuild ties after a diplomatic rift in 2025, when Niger recalled its ambassador to Algiers. Algerias upper house of parliament held an emergency session on Sunday to vote on the proposed deployment.

According to AFP news agency, 148 of the 174 senators voted in favour. It was later backed house of parliament.

## Why This Matters

But, the timing and number of soldiers to be deployed have not been just dropped. The move would mark the first use of a 2020 constitutional provision allowing overseas military deployments and a significant shift in Algerias long-standing policy against sending forces abroad. "We are not sending our soldiers to wage war in Niger, but for a mission to secure economic infrastructure," Algerian senator Belkacem Boulghiti told reporters after the vote.
