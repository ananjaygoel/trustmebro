## The Details

Having effectively began day four on 40-4, South Africa batted on and on against Australia in Cape Town to move from a position where defeat looked likely to one where a series victory is a real possibility. In being bowled out for 387 in the final over - with Ryan Rickelton making 101 and Wiaan Mulder last man out for a brilliant 115 - South Africa set the tourists a target of 288 for victory.

WinViz - the tool analysts CricViz use to predict such results - gives the hosts a 54% chance of victory. If only things were that simple… History a good omen for Australia Australias target is such that should they chase it on day five, it would be the second-highest fourth-innings chase in the 62 previous Tests on this famous ground.

## Why This Matters

Thats a good omen for Australia. They chased 331 for a four-wicket win in an epic Test against the Proteas in 2002, when Ricky Ponting hit a brilliant 100 not out. There have been five other scores above 288 in the fourth innings at Newlands - all of which were made in defeats or when salvaging a draw.
