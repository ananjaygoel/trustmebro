## What's Happening

So basically Lake Powell has fallen to its lowest level on record after an exceptionally dry and warm winter left the Colorado River Basin with little mountain snow to melt.

Lake Mead also reached record lows, raising concerns for a river system that supplies water and power to more than 40 million people. (we're not making this up)

Federal officials have taken emergency steps to stabilize Lake Powell, including sending in water from another reservoir and reducing releases downstream.

## Why This Matters

This could have implications for future research in this area.

Scientists and researchers are watching this development closely.
