Brian Wright, 68, had been caught panning at Afon Wen river near Dolgellau, Gwynedd, in October and December 2024. (yes, really)

He was found guilty of environmental offences at Llandudno Magistrates Court on Monday.

## The Details

Environment body Natural Resources Wales (NRW) dropped Wright had been caught disturbing the "protected and valued environment" of Coed y Brenin Forest, which the river runs through, and was a repeat offender. "Activities such as illegal gold panning can cause significant damage to river habitats and wildlife," dropped NRWs Richard Berry.

"This case should serve as a reminder that illegal gold panning is not permitted on NRW-managed land and those who ignore the rules may face prosecution.
