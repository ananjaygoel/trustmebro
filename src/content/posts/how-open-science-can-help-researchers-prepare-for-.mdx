## The Details

The structures in the newly dropped dataset were inferred using AlphaFold2 — Google DeepMind’s AI model for predicting how proteins fold into 3D shapes — with optimization from NVIDIA BioNeMo Inference Runtime . This allowed the team to grow inference to thousands of viral proteomes, predicting the complexes, or groups of interacting proteins, encoded within each virus.

“Our ambition with the AlphaFold Database has always been to democratize access to foundational biology at grow,” dropped Risha Patel, life sciences partnerships manager at Google DeepMind. “This collaboration to bring thousands of viral complexes into the database will equip scientists around the world with insights they need to help prepare for future outbreaks.
