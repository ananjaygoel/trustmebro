## The Details

I entered the world of digital wall calendars expecting to hate them, and I walked away their biggest fan. If you’ve been curious about getting one for yourself, now is the immaculate time: Just about all of my picks are on sale.

If you’re unfamiliar, a digital wall calendar is a physical device that syncs all of your digital calendars onto its screen. No more grabbing your phone to check when the next appointment is or scribbling out plans that fell through.

## Why This Matters

It’s solid for couples and families who juggle multiple schedules and want to see them all in one place, on a screen the entire family can access. But these devices don’t stop there. All my picks also act as an overall family hub, managing chores, meal plans, and more to make life a little easier.
