## The Details

To be clear, the Rays have clinched only a spot in the postseason. They are assured of being no worse than the third wild card team.

Tampa will now try to hold off the New York Yankees and win their first AL East title since 2021. The division title will almost certainly come with a Wild Card Series bye, so there is still a lot at stake the rest of the regular season.

## Why This Matters

The Rays are a top 10 team at preventing runs, though they're only middle of the pack offensively. Depending on your metric of choice, Tampa is either one of the best defensive teams in baseball (defensive runs saved) or middle of the pack (outs above average). No team in baseball has outperformed its run differential more to date than these Rays.
