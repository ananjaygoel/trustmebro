## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, MIT engineers have built an AI tool that forecasts extreme weather without training on historical disaster data.

Kai Chang, a mechanical engineering graduate student, and Professor Themis Sapsis developed the tool. (let that sink in)

It produces maps of events that have not appeared in a regions historical record but remain statistically-possible.
