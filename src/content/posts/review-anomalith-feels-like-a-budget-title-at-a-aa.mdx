## What's Happening

Breaking it down: While the idea behind Anomalith feels novel and FuRyu’s approach to a Control style scenario can be interesting, it feels like a budget game.

Featured Nintendo Switch 2 Review: Anomalith Feels Like a Budget Title at a AAA Price By Jenni Lada Image via FuRyu If Anomalith was a $20 game, I’d be telling folks to give this Control-style shooter a chance and forgive FuRyu for its shortcomings. So while there is some charm to it, a lot of elements add up to make the whole experience not feel quite worth the investment. (wild, right?)

Anomalith is set in an alternate reality in which backrooms situations and potentially hazardous and lethal anomalies can and will appear.

## The Details

In Japan, the Anomaly Zone Countermeasures Office sends agents to investigate and handle such situations, hopefully even rescuing people suffering from these encounters. While Reona Minazuki should be enjoying a high school life, instead the 17-year-old and her best friend become trapped in an Anomalization while at a mall.

Reona survives and comes out of it with special abilities because of the experience. But her friend Arisa is still missing, and so Reona becomes an Anomaly Zone Countermeasures Diver who works to fight back, help others, and see if she can find Arisa again.

## Why This Matters

Right away, all of the inspirations behind Anomalith are evident once you start playing. The Anomaly Zone Countermeasures Office can feel similar to the concept of the Federal Bureau of Control from Control . Once you start seeing the anomalies and unlock the glossary that lets you look through them, it can feel like seeing SCP sorts of entities or Scarlet Nexus sorts of foes.

The gaming community has been watching developments like this closely.

## The Bottom Line

The locations look like something you’d see in Backrooms or other forms of media with liminal spaces. And the concept of these weird places someone can slip into, changes, and reactions can feel like some ideas are pulled from Roadside Picnic and Otherside Picnic .
