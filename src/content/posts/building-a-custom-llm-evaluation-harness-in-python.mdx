## The Details

How to log results in a crash-safe format that decouples scoring from inference, so you can re-score without re-running expensive API calls. Public benchmarks like MMLU or GSM8K measure general capability, but they wont tell you whether your customer support agent follows your refusal policy, or whether your RAG pipeline pulls the right context for your specific domain.

For that, you need something you own. Thats where a custom evaluation harness comes in: a repeatable pipeline built around your test cases and your scoring criteria.

## Why This Matters

In software engineering, a test harness is the scaffolding that feeds inputs to a system, captures outputs, and scores them against a known baseline. Applied to large language models, it does the same thing across a suite of prompts, tracking pass rates, latency, and cost over time. This guide walks through building one from scratch in Python.
