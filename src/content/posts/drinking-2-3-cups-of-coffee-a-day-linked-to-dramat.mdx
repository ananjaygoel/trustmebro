## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, Drinking two to three cups of caffeinated coffee daily was linked to a dramatically lower risk of dementia and better cognitive functioning in a study spanning up to 43 years.

The benefits appeared regardless of genetic risk, but decaffeinated coffee did not show the same association. Science News Two to three cups of caffeinated coffee a day may help keep your brain sharp and lower your risk of dementia, even if you're genetically predisposed. (it feels like chaos)
