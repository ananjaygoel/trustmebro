Burned-Out Rika Can’t Take Care of Herself begins with Rika Tachibana, a veteran teacher at a middle school, pushing herself too hard for 10 years and not enforcing proper boundaries at work.

As the first chapter begins, we see how not only her assigned tasks get to her, but that her position also meant she wrapped up up stuck as badminton coach and has colleagues disrespecting her else’s due-next-day assignment on her. So, she’s been unable to clean her apartment, hasn’t been able to maintain friendships or a social life, and is stuck living on convenience store food.
