## Why This Matters

In PV, your body makes too many red blood cells, which makes the blood thicker than normal. It leads to a higher risk of developing blood clots, which can cause a stroke, heart attack or deep vein thrombosis (DVT). Symptoms can include headaches, confusion, dizziness and blurred vision.
