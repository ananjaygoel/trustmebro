England entered the game staring at a 150-point deficit following Samoa's landslide win in Papua New Guinea earlier on Saturday.

## The Details

They took a sizeable chunk out of that with a 14-try victory but require another big win when they face the Kumuls in Wollongong on Friday, and will also need a favour from Tonga against Samoa on November 1. Mikey Lewis came off the bench to score twice with Matty Ashton, George Williams, Victor Radley, Jez Litten, Jack Wels Brimson also crossing for England while Hugo Savala kicked 26 points.

Brimson was the architect for many of those tries, the full-back shredding France in an awesome attacking display, with Junior Nsemba and Kai Pearce-Paul producing a number of eye-catching carries and offloads in contact. England were met with stiff resistance during the opening quarter as some superb France defending kept them scoreless, but the dam burst on 23 minutes and those early efforts took its toll as they faded badly towards the end of the first half.

## Why This Matters

Brian McDermott's side scored six tries in the final 17 minutes of the first period, Farnworth grabbing three of those to help England into a 36-0 half-time lead. The rout continued in the second half and France's persistence with going short from kick-offs led to a hat-trick try for Young on 62 minutes, while Brimson bagged the try his performance deserved with three minutes remaining.

Players and coaches will be watching how this unfolds closely.
