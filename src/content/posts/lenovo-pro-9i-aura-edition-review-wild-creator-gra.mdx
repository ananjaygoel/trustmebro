## The Details

The Pro 9i's overall design is the definition of understated, making it well-suited for those after a more low-key, business-oriented look. At the same time, though, it's a bit chunky, especially compared to similar creator-focused laptops.

There's a lot of competition in the prosumer Windows 11 laptop space, including Asus' wild and sleek-looking ProArt P16 for example, which makes the Pro 9i Aura Edition difficult to wholeheartedly recommend at its $1,900 starting price. Still, during my several weeks with the Lenovo's Pro 9i, I've walked away impressed with both the laptop's performance and design.
