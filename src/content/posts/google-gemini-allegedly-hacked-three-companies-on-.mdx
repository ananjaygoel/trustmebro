## Why This Matters

The Wall Street Journal reported that, during a test run by a company called Irregular in May, Gemini went rogue and hacked into three external companies without being instructed to do so. According to Google, this was a case of "mistaken identity," and Gemini stopped itself after realizing it had guessed a real company's password. Because of the lack of a real incident, Google decided not to disclose the hack until the WSJ approached the company.
