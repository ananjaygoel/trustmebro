## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, A sea eagle that confounded the experts by surviving with a broken wing has been spotted back above Scotland's west coast.

Miracle eagle with broken wing spotted back above Scottish coast Image source, Stephen Magee Image caption, Kinky has a distinctive crooked wing where the bone has healed By Victoria Gill Science correspondent Published 1 hour ago A sea eagle that confounded the experts adulthood with a badly broken wing has been spotted back in the skies above Scotlands west coast. The bird was dubbed the Isle of Mulls "miracle eagle" RSPB after it fell from its nest as a chick during a storm in 2023. (wild, right?)

The experts that monitor the islands sea eagle population did not expect the male chick to survive, but it has now been spotted three years on - soaring above the island with the distinctive kink in its wing clearly visible.

## The Details

Retired RSPB officer Dave Sexton, whose has worked with the islands sea eagles for more than 20 years, described the sighting as "incredible". Image source, Beth Sheils Image caption, The most recent photograph of Kinky, captured in the south of the Isle of Mull The bird, affectionately nicknamed Kinky because of the kink in its broken wing, was nursed back to health , unusually caring parents.

In an unprecedented display of extended parenting, the two white-tailed parents skipped a years breeding season to continue to tend for their year-old offspring. This latest sighting, though, reveals that the bird survived his first winter alone.
