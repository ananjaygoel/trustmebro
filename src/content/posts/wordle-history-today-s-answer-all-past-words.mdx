## The Details

Wordle presents the player with six rows of five empty boxes . Simply enter a random five-letter word and the site highlights certain letters in green if they are correct, yellow if they are correct but in the wrong spot, or grey if they are not in the word at all.

With this information, players need to try to figure out the word within the six guesses to be successful. These types of guessing games have been around for a long time.

## Why This Matters
