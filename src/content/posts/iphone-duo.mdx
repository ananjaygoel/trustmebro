## What's Happening

Real talk: While the long-awaited foldable iPhone has often been referred to as the iPhone Ultra or the iPhone Fold in rumors, another name making the rounds is iPhone Duo.

There is no actual evidence that Apple will use the iPhone Duo name, but it is certainly a possibility. Apple has used Duo branding in the past, for its PowerBook Duo line of laptops in the 1990s and for its MagSafe Duo Charger in the early 2020s. (wild, right?)

In this case, Duo would refer to the foldable iPhone's dual screens.

In April, former Macworld reporter Filipe Espósito cited a source familiar with Apple's plans who dropped the foldable iPhone will indeed be named iPhone Ultra . Bloomberg 's Mark Gurman dropped many Apple employees refer to the device as the iPhone Ultra within the company, but he has never firmly reported that it will be the official name.
