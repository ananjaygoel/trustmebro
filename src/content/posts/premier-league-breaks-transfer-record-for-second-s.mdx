## What's Happening

Listen up: Premier League clubs break their all-time summer transfer record after spending reaches £3.

English top flight clubs had already pushed well beyond the £3bn barrier when Iliman Ndiayes move from Everton to Manchester City on deadline day for an initial £60m took the amount spent past the previous mark of £3. Enzo Fernandezs move from Chelsea to Manchester City for a joint-British transfer record fee of £125m was then locked in shortly after the transfer window closed.

## The Details

A total of £475m was spent on deadline day itself, up from £391m last year. The total amount spent during the window considerably dwarfs the £1.

13bn spent five years ago and underlines the power top English clubs have to attract talent, according to figures provided to BBC Sport , founder of FootballTransfers. ## Why This Matters

In fact, this summer window featured four of the top flights five most expensive ever deals. Fernandez was the headline move, surpassing the £116m Manchester City spent earlier in the window to sign Nottingham Forest midfielder Elliot Anderson, while Chelsea paid £117m to sign Morgan Rogers from Aston Villa. Meanwhile, Liverpool paid an initial £106m , which could rise to £123m, for Paris St-Germain and France forward Bradley Barcola.
