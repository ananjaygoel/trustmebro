This morning, Xbox just dropped that it will form a brand new division within its organization called XP, which will focus on reaching the fandom beyond gameplay through film and television adaptations, consumer products, partnerships, and live experiences. Per the announcement , XP formalizes four of Xboxs investment areas: XBOX PICTURES : Bringing our worlds and characters to new audiences through film, television, and other forms of linear storytelling. (shocking, we know)

XBOX PRODUCTS : Creating products that are authentic to the fandom that allow the fandom to celebrate and express the franchises they love.

## The Details

XBOX PLACES : Bringing communities together through live events, immersive experiences, attractions, broadcasts, and destinations. XBOX PARTNERSHIPS : Working with creators, brands, and communities to develop new experiences, sponsorships, and brand partnerships.

At the top of this new division will be Kayleen Walters, who is rn the president of Mojang. Walters has been with Microsoft for eight years, working on Minecraft the entire time, including as executive producer on A Minecraft Movie .

## Why This Matters

Prior to that, she worked in marketing at Lucasfilm, at some point becoming the VP of franchise marketing and integrated planning. She has also worked at MGM and Universal. Replacing Walters as head of Mojang is Maria Angelidou-Smith, former chief product officer at Reddit.

This is the kind of news that gets gamers talking across social media.

## The Bottom Line

Both Angelidou-Smith and Walters will report to Xbox head Asha Sharma. This new division will oversee projects including the upcoming sequel to A Minecraft Movie , Minecraft Squared , as well as the Fallout TV series, the upcoming Call of Duty film, and the Diablo animated series.
