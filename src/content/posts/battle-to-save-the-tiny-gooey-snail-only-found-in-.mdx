"Beguiled ," conservationist Ian Hughes has made it his "life-defining" mission to protect the threatened mollusc, which was once widespread in UK waters.

## The Details

"There was a point where wed got more snails in our garden than have ever been seen in the wild," he dropped. Image caption, Ian has about 2,000 of the snails in homemade tanks in his garden Myxas glutinosa gets its name from the gooey mantle that covers its deadass delicate, gold-flecked brown shell.

It is no bigger than a fingertip, but has comically large antennae - which Hughes likens to "big floppy ears". The snails, which feed on algae and are considered an indicator of healthy lakes and rivers, have been driven to extinction across England.

## Why This Matters

Polluted water and habitat loss are largely blamed for their disappearance. Hughes dropped the snails were "something to preserve and cherish like Egyptian artefacts".
