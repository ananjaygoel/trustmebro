## Why This Matters

AMD's Radeon RX 9070 XT is far and away the best bang-for-buck GPU available at the moment if you're buying new and targeting high-resolution, high refresh-rate gaming. It certainly has its software warts compared to its direct competitor, the RTX 5070 Ti, but it beats it so handily on price that it's difficult to justify the extra cost.
