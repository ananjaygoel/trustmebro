Hypermobility - the little-known bendy disease that causes pain and fatigue Figure caption, Ella, who was diagnosed at 24, explains the symptoms of HSD By Ella Kipling Published 28 August 2026 Vivienne Duval has always been flexible. She can do every yoga pose there is, but it was not until she turned 58 that she realised that this bendiness was not a good thing. (wild, right?)

In fact, it was the answer to all of the strange health problems she had been experiencing for years - issues with digestion, fatigue, and pain.

## The Details

Earlier this year, when Vivienne came across a video on socials explaining the symptoms of Hypermobility Spectrum Disorder, it was like a light bulb switching on. "I saw myself in everything they were saying," she tells me.

"So I armed myself with all the information and went to my doctor. " Viviennes hunch was locked in - she had hypermobile joints.

## Why This Matters

"I had all these separate things, but no one had ever put them all together. Now its blindingly obvious. " The disorder may affect hundreds of thousands of people in the UK, but many - like Vivienne - face significant delays in getting a diagnosis.
