The two original stars of The Hunger Games franchise walked the red carpet and posed for photos more than a decade after the press tour for The Hunger Games: Mockingjay Part 2 concluded their four-movie run.

## The Details

The Sunrise on the Reaping world premiere turned out to be a reunion of sorts for the entire Hunger Games franchise. Former cast members such as Elizabeth Banks (Effie Trinket), Amandla Stenberg (Rue), Jena Malone (Johanna Mason) and Willow Shields (Primrose Everdeen) made appearances on the carpet.

Rachel Zegler also posted for photos after headlining the previous prequel movie, The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, as Lucy Gray Baird, as did her co-star Tom Blyth, who played a young President Snow.
