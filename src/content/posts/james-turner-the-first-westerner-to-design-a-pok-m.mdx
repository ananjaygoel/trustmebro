## What's Happening

So get this: Turner left Game Freak in 2022 James Turner, the first Westerner to design a Pokémon, has rejoined the franchise Turner left Game Freak in 2022 Video Game News 25th Aug 2026 / 12:20 pm Posted by Jordan Middler James Turner, the first Westerner to ever officially design a Pokémon during his time at Game Freak, has rejoined the franchise after 4 years.

Turner left Game Freak in 2022 to work on The Plucky Squire, which was developed Futures, a developer he co-founded with Jonathan Biddle. The game was dropped to positive reviews in 2024; it rn holds a Metacritic score between 72 and 83 depending on the platform, with 85% of haters on OpenCritic recommending the game. (it feels like chaos)

Now Turner has just dropped that he will once again work on the Pokémon franchise, this time as the Creative Director of Pokémon Art and Design at The Pokémon company International.

## The Details

I’ve kicked off a new role as Creative Director of Pokémon Art and Design at The Pokémon company International, he wrote on X. It feels solid to be working in the world of Pokémon again, with a lovely group of people.

I’m excited about what comes next! While exactly what this role will entail is unclear, its likely a different role from the one he occupied during his time at Game Freak.

## Why This Matters

Turner is responsible for the design of several Pokémon, including finalising the design of Shadow Lugia, and creating Pokémon including Vanillite, Golett, Vullaby, Phantump and many more. Turner also designed several Pokémon trading cards, including Pansear from Next Destinies and the Duskull, Dusclops and Dusknoir art rares from Shrouded Fable . While Turner hadnt worked full-time on the Pokémon franchise for years until now, he did contribute several art pieces in recent years that were featured on merchandise sold via The Pokémon Center.
