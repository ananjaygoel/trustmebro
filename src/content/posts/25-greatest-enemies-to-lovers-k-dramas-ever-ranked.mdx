## The Details

Sure, straightforward romances such as friends-to-lovers stories can be sweet, but nothing quite beats the thrill of seeing two individuals who supposedly despise each other reluctantly fall in love. The initial denial followed and the growing tension during all the bickering make for a engaging story, which is why the enemies-to-lovers trope has lived on for so long, and K-dramas have also seen every variant of it by now.
