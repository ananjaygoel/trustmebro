## Why This Matters

To address that challenge, Ofcom has - plus to traditional forms of ID - recommended four methods that it says would be "highly effective" at gauging peoples ages: photo ID matching, where relevant information on a photo ID document such as a passport is checked against a live image taken during an age check banking-based checks, which involves using the information a financial institution has on record regarding a users age face age estimation technology (which the regulator argues has just improved) digital ID services, which enable people to digitally prove who they are or provide information about themselves, such as their age The BBC has asked three of the UKs biggest banks whether they would have a process for assisting with socials requests for age verification if it did become an official measure.
