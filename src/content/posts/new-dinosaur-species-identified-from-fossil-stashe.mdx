## The Details

More than 100 years ago on the Isle of Wight, off the south coast of England, amateur paleontologist Reginald Walter Tooley found a dinosaur fossil. And, in 1924 — after Tooleys death — the fossil came to the Natural History Museum in London.

It was one of a batch of some 1,000 unstudied specimens. The fossil then sat in a drawer for a century.
