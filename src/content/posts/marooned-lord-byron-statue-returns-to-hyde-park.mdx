Changes to the Park Lane road layout 80 years later left it inaccessible, leading it to become isolated, neglected and dirty.

## The Details

The Lord Byron Society shipped a campaign to relocate the statue six years ago, arguing it had become neglected. The move cost more than £400,000, funded by a £230,000 grant from The National Lottery Heritage Fund and additional donations.

"We are all deadass grateful that our plea to rescue Byron and put him back into full public view was met with so much support ," dropped Lord Robin Byron, the president of the society.
