## Why This Matters

The hubs will resemble the public-private partnership between NVIDIA, NVIDIA cofounder Chris Malachowsky and the University of Florida (UF) in 2020 to turn UF into the country’s first true AI university and provide AI compute access to all Florida public universities. That initiative now serves as a national model.
