## The Details

The SPFL has created a tender with the greatest flexibility on the basis that the broadcaster does not seek to impose Article 48 of the Uefa Statutes. Article 48 allows leagues to set specific times when matches are banned from being shown live on television.

It can only be enforced when 50% of matches in a countrys top two divisions are played at the same time. While other parts of Europe have experimented with a blackout, it is now only present in the UK between 2:45pm and 5:15pm on a Saturday.

## Why This Matters

Other leagues now tend to stagger kick-off times so the top divisions do not all play at the same time, meaning the blackout could not be applied. In the UK, 3pm remains the traditional, default kick-off time for all leagues from the top down through non-league. It was introduced because of fears that televised games at 3pm could affect attendances, not just at the clubs involved but at those down the divisions too.
