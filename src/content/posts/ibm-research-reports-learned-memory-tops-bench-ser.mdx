## Why This Matters

The method analyzes an agent’s attempts on training tasks and uses the same model to distill successes and failures into short behavioral rules, covering situations such as authenticating a customer, confirming a transaction, or handling a return. According to the post, the same model powers both guideline extraction and the agent itself, and the process requires no weight updates or human annotation. The team before demonstrated the approach on coding agents; the new study applies it to customer service.
