## The Details

Todays top story: Fifty-three years ago today, on , three astronauts shipped toward Americas first space station, Skylab. They became the third and final crew to live aboard Skylab.

Gerald Carr, Edward Gibson and William Pogue were all space rookies. Even so, they went on to set a record, staying in space for 84 days.

## Why This Matters

So what does Skylab have to do with the sun? The station carried a solar observatory called the Apollo Telescope Mount, with eight instruments aimed at our star. The crew used it to capture about 75,000 new images of the sun in X-ray, ultraviolet and visible light.

The scientific community tends to find developments like this significant.

## Key Takeaways

- Gibson was a solar physicist, and he kept a close watch on the telescope controls.
- He watched and captured images of a solar flare from the moment it began until it faded away, the first time anyone had ever done that.
- So, scientists learned a solid deal about how flares work.
