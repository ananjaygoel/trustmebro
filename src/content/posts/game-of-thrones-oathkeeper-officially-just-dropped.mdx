## Why This Matters

Weapons such as Ned Stark’s Ice and Daemon Targaryen’s Dark Sister carry histories and associations that make them feel like enduring pieces of the world rather than mere tools of combat. In most cases, the weapon's history serves to mirror the character that wields it, and arguably no weapon depicts this connection more symbolically than Brienne of Tarth's iconic Oathkeeper.
