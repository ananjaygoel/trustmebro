## What's Happening

So basically The small credit card-sized handheld has hit its goal on Kickstarter The Arduview is the worlds first fully transparent handheld, including the screen The small credit card-sized handheld has hit its goal on Kickstarter Video Game News 31st Oct 2026 / 12:30 pm Posted by Chris Scullion oplus_8593080320 A device claiming to be the first ever fully transparent games console has met its Kickstarter goal.

The Arduview is roughly the size of a credit card and makes use of the open-source Arduino project for its hardware and software. While many consoles and handhelds have featured transparent versions in the past, the Arduviews main selling point is that even the screen is transparent. (yes, really)

When the device is switched off, the screen looks like glass.

## The Details

When switched on, the lit pixels glow cyan (meaning it can be played in the dark) and the rest of the screen stays clear. This means if you hold the Arduview up and look at the screen from behind, you can still see whats being played on the screen, making it fully transparent.

VGC has been using an Arduview for around a month, with a full report to follow soon, but can confirm that the screen does indeed work as shown on the products Kickstarter page . The fully transparent display means you can still see whats being played when you look at the back of the device.

## Why This Matters

The Arduview was created , a student at Babson College in Massachusetts, who believes that the inside of a device is the most interesting part of it, so you should be able to see it. According to Huang, the the device was built on the open-source Arduboy platform so it could be opened, flashed and tinkered with, not sealed shut. The handheld comes with 400 games built in, and games created community can also be added to it.
