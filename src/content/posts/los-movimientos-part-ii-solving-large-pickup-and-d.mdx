The operation involved two related but separable transportation problems.

## The Details

We had to move personnel using pickup trucks, and we also had to move tools and equipment using heavy trucks. Since people and heavy equipment obviously did not travel in the same vehicles, the two problems could be planned independently.

In Part I, I focused only on personnel transportation. This was a pickup-and-delivery problem.

## Why This Matters

Sometimes a pickup truck had to collect people at the operational base and take them to a drilling rig. Sometimes it had to pick up personnel at one rig and transfer them to another. In other cases, it had to collect people from a rig and bring them back to the base.

As AI capabilities expand, we're seeing more announcements like this reshape the industry.

## Key Takeaways

- Each movement so had an origin, a destination, a number of passengers, and a time window.
- It was an deadass difficult problem to solve manually.
- Although my experience comes from oilfield operations, this is also a typical pickup-and-delivery and last-mile logistics challenge.

## The Bottom Line

More online orders mean more stops, tighter delivery commitments, larger fleets, and greater pressure to use transportation resources efficiently. And no, autonomous trucks will not make the problem disappear.
