## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, There are now 25 classic Xbox games available on PC, and four have achievements 21 original Xbox games are now on PC via backward compatibility There are now 25 classic Xbox games available on PC, and four have achievements 12th Nov 2026 / 10:01 pm Posted by Rhiannon Bevan Xbox has added 21 new original Xbox games to its backwards compatibility catalogue on PC.

The Elder Scrolls 3: Morrowind, Star Wars Battlefront, Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic, TimeSplitters 2, and more have been added to the catalogue, bringing the roster up to 25 on PC . Four of the games available on the service will also have unlockable achievements. (shocking, we know)
