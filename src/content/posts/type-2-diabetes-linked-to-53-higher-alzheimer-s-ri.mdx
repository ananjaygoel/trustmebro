## What's Happening

So basically People with type 2 diabetes face a 53% higher risk of developing Alzheimer’s disease, according to a major new review of 40 studies.

Researchers estimate that nearly five out of every 1,000 people with type 2 diabetes develop Alzheimer’s each year. (wild, right?)

By focusing specifically on these two conditions, the study offers a clearer understanding of a potentially serious connection between metabolic health and brain health.
