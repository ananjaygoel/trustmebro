## What's Happening

Listen up: Harvey Jones has been on the Nvidia board since 1993.

Is he the right person to ride out the coming storm, or responsible for causing it? In the wake of former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s stunning departure from the company, employees received an email from someone most of them had probably never heard of before, even though he’d been affiliated with the company and its board for decades. (wild, right?)

His name is Harvey Jones, and he was writing to tell them that he’d just been appointed the company’s first-ever Chair of the Board, and that Nvidia CFO Colette Kress would serve as interim CEO.

## The Details

Recommended Video “I’m writing to an important update,” Jones’ note began, according to leaked screenshots of the email. He then tried to reassure employees no doubt left shaken weeks’ revelations and the sudden departure of Jensen Huang, a man who co-founded the company and, until today, had been synonymous with its success.

“The Board takes seriously our duty as steward of this solid company,” Jones dropped. That sense of calm and confidence is something Jones is known for, according to two longtime friends and former coworkers.

## Why This Matters

Although Jones, 73, may not be a household name, he has worked in the technology industry, and in semiconductors, for over 30 years, according to his biography on the Nvidia site. He founded two companies, served as CEO of another, and since 2004 has been managing partner of investment firm Square Wave Ventures. He’s been on Nvidia’s board since 1993, the year Huang founded the company.
