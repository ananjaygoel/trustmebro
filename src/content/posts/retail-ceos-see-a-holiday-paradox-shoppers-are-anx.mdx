As consumer and retail CEOs see the holiday season inching closer, they’re trying to figure out whether U. Users will keep up their healthy pace of shopping—even in the face of all political and economic tumult—or finally pull back.

## Why This Matters

There’s one thing chief executives agree on: the U. Consumer is anxious and their spending can’t be taken for granted.
