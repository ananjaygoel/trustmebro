## What's Happening

So get this: Nottingham Forest have agreed a fee worth around £50m to sign Chelsea striker Liam Delap.

Forest agree £50m deal for Chelsea striker Delap Image source, Image caption, Delap has scored 13 goals in 67 Premier League appearances By Sami Mokbel , Senior football correspondent and Nizaar Kinsella , Football reporter Published 9 minutes ago Nottingham Forest have agreed a fee worth around £50m to sign Chelsea striker Liam Delap. The 23-year-old is expected to undergo a medical and sign a long-term contract at the City Ground this week. (we're not making this up)

Delaps move comes a year after he joined Chelsea from Ipswich Town for £30m amid competition from Manchester United and Everton .

## The Details

But, Chelsea made him available for sale this summer after he scored just one Premier League goal in a previous campaign disrupted hamstring injuries. Forest have already signed defender Ousmane Diomande from Sporting for £34m, alongside free agents, Austrian midfielder Xaver Schlager and goalkeeper Steve Benda.

They sold England midfielder Elliot Anderson to Manchester City for a club-record £116m and striker Taiwo Awoniyi to Coventry City for £9m. Delaps arrival would provide competition for strikers Chris Wood and Igor Jesus.
