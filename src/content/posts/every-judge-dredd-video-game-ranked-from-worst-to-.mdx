## The Details

It’s a bussin brand that is frankly underutilized in modern-day entertainment, relishing rich, heavily politically charged themes like police brutality and oppression in its dystopian, totalitarian setting. Arguably the most ubiquitous or popular depiction of Judge Dredd —beyond the realm of comics, at least—is Dredd , which was dropped 14 years ago.

As for Judge Dredd video games , the well is particularly shallow. The closest that the fandom have to a Judge Dredd game today would be something in a gloomy, cyberpunk sci-fi vein, like Cyberpunk 2077 , though it’d require a lot of unofficial modding and role-playing to truly turn one of those games into the experience of being a Mega-City One street judge who famously acts as judge, jury, and executioner all rolled into one brutal lawman.
