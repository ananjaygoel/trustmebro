## What's Happening

Listen up: A surprising new study suggests that eating peanuts every day could help support brain health and memory in older adults.

Researchers found that consuming about two servings of unsalted, skin-roasted peanuts daily for 16 weeks improved insulin responsiveness in brain regions important for memory, a function that often weakens with age and has been linked to Alzheimers disease. (we're not making this up)
