## Why This Matters

Modeling Industrial Supply Chains Atmospheric AI’s approach begins with the physical processes used to make materials, rather than depending on suppliers to disclose internal operating data. Its models incorporate chemical engineering, plant economics and industrial processes, including relationships among feedstocks, energy, production costs and emissions. The connected system covers oil fields and pipelines, refineries, chemical plants, terminals and shipping routes.
