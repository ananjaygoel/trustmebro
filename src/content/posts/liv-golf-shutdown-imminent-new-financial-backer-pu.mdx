## What's Happening

Real talk: BC Partners has reportedly walked away from its $300 million rescue plan because of a lack of player commitments Just over six months after losing its original financial backer, LIV Golf has reportedly seen its potential savior fall as private capital firm BC Partners has decided to drop its bid to keep the league running.

BC Partners had before committed upwards of $300 million to keep LIV Golf afloat with the circuit aiming to reinvent itself in the wake of Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund deciding to no longer finance the upstart league that once aimed to put the PGA Tour out of business. ## The Details

The capital firm was expecting at least twice as many player commitments. LIV Golf now may have no choice but to liquidate its assets and shutter the league entirely.
