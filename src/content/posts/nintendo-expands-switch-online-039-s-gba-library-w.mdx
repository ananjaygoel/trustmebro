## What's Happening

Let's talk about Monster Rancher, Metal Slug & Kururin.

Nintendo has today expanded its Switch Online 'Nintendo Classics' library with three more Game Boy Advance titles. The games added to this particular collection include Tecmo's 2001 life-sim spin-off Monster Rancher Advance, SNK's 2004 run and gun Metal Slug Advance, and the 2002 puzzle game Kururin Paradise by Eighting. (yes, really)

Japan has received the same titles this week.
