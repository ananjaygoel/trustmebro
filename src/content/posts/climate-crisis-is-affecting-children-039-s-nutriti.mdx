## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, Wildfires, floods and other extreme weather events are no longer the only ways climate change affects children around the world.

Increasingly, it also affects their diets. (shocking, we know)

Droughts reduce agricultural production, floods cut off roads and disrupt supply chains, and rising prices limit families access to fruits, vegetables and other fresh foods.

## Why This Matters

The scientific community tends to find developments like this significant.

This could have implications for future research in this area.
