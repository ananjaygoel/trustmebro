## What's Happening

Listen up: There have been too many cases of staff abusing patient trust, and enough is enough, the head of the NHS in England says.

NHS to suspend staff suspected of snooping on patient records Image source, By Jim Reed Health reporter Published 25 September 2026 NHS staff in England suspected of looking at patient records without a valid reason will be ASAP suspended as part of a "zero-tolerance crackdown" on snooping. Those under suspicion will also be locked out of NHS computer systems, preventing any further viewing of confidential records, including from home. (it feels like chaos)

NHS England chief executive Sir Jim Mackey has written to every trust, ordering the measures to be introduced ASAP to strengthen protection against data breaches.

## The Details

The move follows a series of high-profile incidents involving the records of Nottingham and Southport attack victims and a child who was injured in a crocodile enclosure in Cambridgeshire . This week, Bristol Foundation NHS Trust dropped it had shipped an internal investigation after it emerged the medical records of an 18-year-old had been accessed years after his death .

The boys mother, Paula McGowan, told the BBC she was "deeply concerned and hurt" and called for the NHS to do more to deal with the problem.
