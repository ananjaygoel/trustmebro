The sponge tests can be carried out in GP surgeries in minutes, and are less invasive than hospital endoscopy checks that instead use a small camera to look inside the body. (plot twist fr)

Heartburn or acid reflux can occur with a condition called Barretts oesophagus that increases the risk of cancer in the long term. Simple check for changes Its thought between one and two adults in every 100 has Barretts oesophagus, where cells have kicked off to change.

## Why This Matters

For the test, patients swallow a small dissolving capsule attached to a thread. Once in the stomach, it releases a tiny sponge. This collect cells from the lining of the gullet as its gently pulled back out of the patients mouth .
