## The Details

Todays top story: A small coronal hole high-speed stream could brush Earth early today. Forecasters at both NOAA and the UK Met Office give a slight chance of G1 (minor) geomagnetic storm conditions if the connection materializes.

That would put aurora on the table for observers at high latitudes, including northern Scotland and similar geomagnetic latitudes. Meanwhile, the sun itself is running quiet.

## Why This Matters

Solar activity stayed at low levels over the past day, with only C-class and B-class flares. The most active region, AR4534, has now rotated behind the west limb. Observers detected no Earth-directed coronal mass ejections (CMEs).

This could have implications for future research in this area.
