## Why This Matters

It has been officially described as a one-in-75-year event when the river flooded more than 400 properties in Monmouth during Storm Claudia - and overwhelmed the defences that were built 35 years ago to protect the centre of town. Jean, like many in the small town near the Wales and England border, was rescued and after sleeping the night on her neighbours chair sought temporary sanctuary in Monmouths Wetherspoons as she had no close family to look after her.
