Cancer became undetectable on scans in one in 12 of the patients given daraxonrasib along with chemotherapy in early medical trials.

## The Details

Tumours stopped growing in almost all of the 61 participants who had just been diagnosed with advanced pancreatic cancer that had spread to other parts of the body. But doctors stress it is unclear how long it might keep the disease at bay and should not be seen as a cure.

Insanely exciting Cancer Research UK called it "the biggest leap forward in decades" for pancreatic cancer patients. The charitys Dr Sam Godfrey told BBC News that the results were insanely exciting.
