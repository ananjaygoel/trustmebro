## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, The happy-go-lucky bigwigs at Epic Games have instructed the creators of an unannounced shooter that features Marvel and Star Wars characters to complete their project using an "AI first" production model, according to a report, with generated art and code.

The Disney collaboration in question was allegedly due for release in March, but has now been delayed indefinitely. The game's makers are, we're told, being ordered to make heavier use of Anthropic's Claude and similar tools. (wild, right?)

## The Details

The report in question is from Bloomberg , who cite "people familiar with the project", together with an alleged internal message from the game's director. No more hand-written code, the director is dropped to have told the devs, with all art to be generated with full AI workflows.

Epic have, in general, bet big on generative AI. The tech is central to the next iteration of Unreal Engine , much to the displeasure of many game developers .

## Why This Matters

Epic already use the stuff like a bike pump to inflate Fortnite character designs into something like a 3D model . They've also deployed it to voice-act Darth Vader, earning the ire of unions . Meanwhile, company CEO Tim Sweeney keeps complaining about Valve requiring genAI disclosures on Steam , evidently sensing an opportunity to lure AI-curious developers away to the perpetually ailing Epic Games Store.

The gaming community has been watching developments like this closely.

## Key Takeaways

- The rush to make hay out of genAI comes as Epic's moneyhorse Fortnite starts to run out of kick.
