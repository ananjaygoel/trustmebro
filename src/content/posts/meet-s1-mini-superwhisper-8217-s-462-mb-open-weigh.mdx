## The Details

The one that is quite interesting outside the app is S1-mini , dropped with open weights on Hugging Face. 6B text normalizer, not a transcriber and not a chat model.

It sits after automatic speech recognition and rewrites raw transcripts as clean written text: fillers removed, self-corrections resolved to what the speaker landed on, punctuation and capitalization applied, and spoken numbers, dates, currency, and email addresses rendered in written form. It is fine-tuned from Qwen/Qwen3-0.

## Why This Matters

6B , covers English only in release v1, and is steered entirely by a three-axis control line placed above the transcript. 8% token accuracy on a held-out set of 7,519 cases, measured greedy on the quantized build. S1-mini is Hugging Face under Apache 2.
