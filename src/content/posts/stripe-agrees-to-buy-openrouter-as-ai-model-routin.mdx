## What's Happening

Okay so Stripe has agreed to acquire OpenRouter, an AI model-routing platform that gives developers access to hundreds of models through a single interface.

The deal adds model selection and routing to Stripe’s existing work around AI usage and token-based billing. (plot twist fr)

OpenRouter supports more than 400 models from over 80 providers, according to Stripe.
