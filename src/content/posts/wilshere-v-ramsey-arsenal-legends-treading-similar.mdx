## The Details

The image of the pair celebrating Ramseys extra-time winner together in the 2014 FA Cup final against Hull City to end Arsenals nine-year trophy drought at the time will forever be ingrained in the north London clubs history. They were teenagers when they made their Premier League debuts for the Gunners on the same day just two minutes apart as substitutes in a 4-0 win at Blackburn in September 2008.

Aged just 16 and 17, each were considered hot prospects who would go on to have fire careers - and so it proved in many ways, even if both were restricted the way. Now 34 and 35, their paths cross again under different circumstances but many similarities much still remain.

## Why This Matters

They are both in League One in their first permanent managerial jobs. Both their sides are in fine form and vying for promotion to the Championship. It will be the first time they have met as permanent managers when Wilsheres Luton Town host Ramseys Oxford United at Kenilworth Road on Saturday (15:00 GMT).

## The Bottom Line

"Obviously it will be nice to see Jack again, but its not about us," Ramsey told BBC Radio Oxford. Except it is about them a little bit, given their 101 appearances in 10 years together in the Arsenal midfield - and as two young managers who are following almost identical paths.
