## Why This Matters

It's sleek, powerful, has a solid display, and runs everything you could want from the Google Play store, and that last part is exactly why I found it to be such a puzzling device. There are so few titles in the Play Store that justify the hardware within, and during my weeks of hands-on testing with the Legion Tab Gen 5, I was challenged to find a reason why this tablet would be worth its near $850 price tag for anyone outside the niche of Android gamers.
