## The Details

The Trill are first introduced in the "Star Trek: The Next Generation" episode "The Host," wherein Dr. Beverly Crusher (Gates McFadden) falls in love with a Trill ambassador named Odan (Franc Luz).

Partway through that episode, it's spilled that the face and body that Dr. Crusher loved belongs to a mere host being, while his personality and memories all come from a small, sentient worm-like creature that has been surgically implanted in his abdomen.
