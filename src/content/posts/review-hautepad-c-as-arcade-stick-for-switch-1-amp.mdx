Aesthetically, the C-AS is perhaps Cosmox’s boldest design to date. The metallic plate is a shining gold colour, covered with a subtle yet striking pattern made up of the company’s logo.

## Why This Matters

Sounds garish on paper, sure, but it genuinely looks lovely in person. I received the gold version for review, but it’s also available in grey and purple. You've also got the usual LCD screen in the top left and some neat RGB lighting around the buttons and main body.

## Key Takeaways

- The stick itself feels good, but it needs a bit of work for future iterations.
- Cosmox has endeavoured to keep the C-AS as thin as possible, which means the stick isn’t made in the same way as industry-standard levers like Sanwa.
