© bubuge12 Since the game’s release in 2016, the items you sell in Stardew Valley have always just…been sent into a void. Sure, Lewis technically picks them up every night, but you never actually see him collecting or using the items you harvest, cook, and gather, so they can feel a bit pointless at times . Over the past six months, but, a modder has been working to change that with an ambitious new project.

## The Details

On Saturday, Nexus Mods user bubuge12 uploaded a mod titled Farmers Store , which lets players manage their own supermarket. Per the game’s description, it includes “an Overcooked -style kitchen” alongside stock management, employee hiring, advertising, and compatibility with several of Stardew’s most popular mods, with multiplayer support rn on its way.

Best of all, though, the Valley’s villagers actually come to your store to buy your items! That’s a lot to put together in just six months!

## Why This Matters

And, according to comments on Nexus Mods, it all works pretty well. There are, unfortunately, a few caveats here. The mod is only partially available in English, as text in assets is limited to Chinese.

The gaming community has been watching developments like this closely.

## Key Takeaways

- One more thing: some of the mod’s dialogue was generated by AI.
- It at least seems like there aren’t any new storylines or characters—just flavor text for things like villagers’ reactions.
- And bubuge12 did state that “only about 20% of the text is AI-generated” and that they’re planning to replace it with their own work in the future.
