## The Details

Sounds stressful, but the game's modelling of time is quite forgiving : each day is broken into chrono-locked segments, with time only passing when you make certain choices. Also, you can "bend" time people's teeth to the Tooth Fairy.

The fairies in question are called Sanzhana. They look like chubby half-human moths, with blue skin and whispering wings.

## Why This Matters
