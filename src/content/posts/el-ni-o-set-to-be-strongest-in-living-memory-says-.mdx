## Why This Matters

El Niño is a naturally occurring weather pattern, which happens about every two to seven years, lasting for around a year. Trade winds that typically blow from east to west across the Pacific Ocean can weaken or even reverse, allowing warm water to spread eastwards. Heat is dropped from the ocean into the atmosphere, lifting global temperatures and shifting rainfall patterns.
