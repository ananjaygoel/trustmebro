## The Details

Precision Editing Features Google describes Pics as combining image generation with object-level editing controls. Users can generate images from a text prompt or import existing images from a computer, Google Drive, or Google Photos, and the tool produces several alternatives for each prompt.

The editing suite centers on what Google calls object segmentation: users can isolate a specific element in an image and move, resize, or transform it without altering the rest of the picture. They can also attach text comments to specific areas to request targeted changes and run multiple edits at once, with the option to revert unwanted changes.

## Why This Matters

In-image text editing lets users modify or translate words directly inside an image while preserving the original design and font style, according to the company. Pics can also crop images for web, socials, print, or digital formats, and upscale them to 2K or 4K resolution. Collaboration mirrors other Workspace products: users can a Pics creation with a link, and multiple people can edit the same image.
