## The Details

Gills agent Mel Kenyon dropped his work had a "rare emotional delicacy" that was "beautifully observed and finely tuned". Gill is closely associated with his work at the Royal Court in London, where he is credited with introducing the plays of English writer DH Lawrence to the theatrical world.

Born in Cardiff on 7 September 1939, he was raised in a working-class community and remained "fiercely proud of his Welsh heritage throughout his life", according to an obituary written Casarotto Ramsay & Associates. He pursued an acting career from the age of 18 before moving into theatre, most closely associated with the Royal Court, where he championed three Lawrence plays A Colliers Friday Night, The Daughter-in-Law and The Widowing of Mrs Holroyd.
