## The Details

At first glance, the strange new discovery—a nearly eight-inch rod perforated with four small holes—seemed comparatively unspectacular. But it has since spilled far more about the lives of Europe’s prehistoric hunter-gatherers than its unassuming appearance might have suggested.

Fashioned from the tusk of a mammoth more than 35,000 years ago, this baton—a type of artifact called a lochstab —was initially interpreted as a symbolic object associated with prestige or power. But Nicholas Conard , whose team found out the item, wasn’t so sure.
