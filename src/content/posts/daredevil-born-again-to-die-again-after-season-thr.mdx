## The Details

The Hollywood Reporter reports Daredevil: Born Again will end after its third season, which production wrapped on about two months ago. The news, THR notes, comes after Marvel quietly split from Daredevil showrunner Dario Scardapane, whose contract expired and was not renewed in the middle of season threes post-production.

Things have never been particularly easygoing for The Man Without Fear. In 2022, Marvel just dropped it was bringing Charlie Cox, who starred as Matt Murdock on Netflixs Daredevil , to the MCU via an ambitious 18-episode first season.

## Why This Matters

But shortly thereafter, the show underwent a complete creative overhaul after shooting six episodes . The 2023 strikes had shut down the industry, giving Marvel time to retool. Originally developed as a total reboot with episodic installments and Daredevils suit not appearing until episode four, the shows head writers, Matt Corman and Chris Ord, were let go, and Scardapane was brought in to make a direct continuation of the Netflix series.
