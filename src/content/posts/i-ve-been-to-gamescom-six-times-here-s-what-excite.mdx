## What's Happening

Listen up: The BBC's Laura Cress gets a first look at the biggest games in Cologne from Witcher 3 to Alien: Isolation 2.

Ive been to Gamescom six times - heres what excited me this year Figure caption, Watch: BBCs Laura Cress on the floor at Gamescom in Cologne By Laura Cress Technology reporter Published 30 August 2026 Gamescom is the largest in-person gaming event in the world, and this year it lived up to expectations with big games on show from The Witcher 3s new expansion, to Alien: Isolation 2. Last year over 350,000 people flocked to the halls of the Koelnmesse in Cologne, Germany to get a chance to see and play the most hotly anticipated games. (wild, right?)

This year that number of attendees is expected to have been even bigger, with the likes of Xbox and Sega showcasing games to the public alongside indie studios with only one or two developers.

## The Details

This was my sixth time attending Gamescom, which never fails to make me excited about the games industry. Here are a few of the big things I got to check out this week.

The Witcher 3: Songs of the Past Image source, CD Projekt Red/Fools Theory Image caption, Gamers will once again play as the gruff-voiced Geralt of Rivia in the new The Witcher 3 expansion When CD Projekt Red just dropped it was making a new expansion for the hugely popular action-role playing game The Witcher 3, it came as a shock. The original, after all, came out 11 years ago.

## Why This Matters

Yet it was clear sitting in the crowd for Opening Night Live on Tuesday that theres still a lot of love for the older game. There were gasps and audible shouts of joy when the team just dropped they were providing a free upgrade to the original. As for the expansion, Songs of the Past will follow the gruff-voiced protagonist Geralt in an adventure discovering more about the past of his close friend Dandelion.

This could have major implications for how we use technology going forward.

## Key Takeaways

- Doug Cockle, who voices Geralt, told me it was a "thrill" to return as The Witcher.
- "I love this character so much...
- Its like slipping into a warm bath, or putting on an old pair of shoes," he dropped.
