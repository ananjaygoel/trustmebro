Instead, the proceedings, which have been called the “ worst COP ever ,” wrapped up Sunday without an overarching agreement, after disputes over fossil fuels and climate finance pushed negotiations two days into overtime. With 2026 now expected to be one of the four hottest years in the climate record, the stakes of the summit were clear.

## The Details

Summer heat waves contributed to 50,000 deaths in Europe , with two-thirds of those linked to human-caused climate change. And a catastrophic glacier collapse in Nepal killed more than 1,500 people in August, with recovery costs estimated at one-tenth of the country’s GDP.

What’s more, the talks come as the world is reportedly certain to exceed the 2015 Paris Agreement’s goal of limiting global warming to 1. 5 degrees Celsius above preindustrial levels.

## Why This Matters

Scientists warn that returning temperatures below that threshold after an overshoot will require rapid and sustained reductions in greenhouse gas emissions, potentially alongside efforts to remove carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. This month’s conference, but, exposed deep divisions over how to achieve those goals. Every COP since 2003 has wrapped up in an agreement, even if the final text had watered-down commitments to appease all countries.

Scientists and researchers are watching this development closely.
