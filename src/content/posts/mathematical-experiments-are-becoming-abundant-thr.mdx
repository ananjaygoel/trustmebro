## The Details

One resisted every route I tried. The other produced a proof candidate: a conventional argument written out in full, exact checks on selected inputs, a teaching guide and a partial formalisation in Lean.

That second outcome is still a candidate. No specialist has reviewed the argument, its novelty is not established, and Lean checks only its algebraic core rather than the complete theorem.

## Why This Matters

I would not publish it as a new theorem in its current state. The pattern I took from the weekend is that mathematical experiments are becoming abundant. The Hadamard project closed 44 precisely defined regions of the search space and audited five standard routes to a nonexistence proof.

As AI capabilities expand, we're seeing more announcements like this reshape the industry.
