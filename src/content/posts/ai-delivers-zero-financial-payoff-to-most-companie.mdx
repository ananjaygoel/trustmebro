## What's Happening

So get this: "Despite massive investments and widespread hype around AI, the technology has delivered zero financial payoff to corporate America outside of tech.

" AI hasn’t benefited the bottom line of any big corporations outside the tech industry, according to a note just published economist at Apollo Global Management (the private equity giant that used to own TechCrunch). (it feels like chaos)

Apollo’s Torsten Slok focuses on what he calls the S&P 493 — the S&P 500, minus Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, Meta, Microsoft, Nvidia and Tesla — finding that profit margins and revenue per worker appear to be flat, with no evidence that AI is improving productivity.

## Why This Matters

This is part of the broader shift happening across the tech industry right now.

Tech companies have been making moves like this as competition heats up.
