## The Details

Pandas rely on a diet of between 70 to 100 pounds of bamboo a day as an adult. When they’re not foraging for food in their mountainous habitat, they are resting and sleeping.

Males can grow to be around 250 pounds and females roughly 220 pounds as adults, and they can live for up to 30 years when in human care. Check out these photos of pandas, most of which are cared for by zookeepers, biologists and conservationists.
