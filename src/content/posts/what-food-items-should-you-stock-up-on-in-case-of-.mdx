## The Details

Environment Secretary Dame Angela Eagle told the Guardian the powerful natural climate phenomenon El Niño would lead to "more extreme storms in this country". Climate change, along with cyber-attacks, disease outbreaks and conflicts can all put a strain on the process that at some point gets food into our shops and homes.
