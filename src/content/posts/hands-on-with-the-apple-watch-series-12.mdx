## Why This Matters

You might not be able to tell a Series 11 apart from a Series 12 when looking at the front, but the back has been updated. Apple says the upgraded sensors make the Apple Watch Series 12 the most accurate wearable for heart rate sensing. The Series 12 has a Health Sensing System that pairs the new sensors with an S11 chip that's faster than the S10 in the prior-generation model.

## Key Takeaways

- It takes heart rate readings every five seconds now and measures heart rate variability as often as every five minutes.
- It's early, but so far we haven't seen the higher sampling rates cause excessive battery drain.
- The Apple Watch Series 12 is still rated for all-day battery life, and charging is faster this year.
- You can get up to 12 hours of normal usage from a 15-minute charge, but you need to use the Apple Watch charger that comes with the Series 12.

## The Bottom Line

The Health Sensing System powers a readiness score that lets you know whether to go all-out on activity or take a rest day. It's based on metrics such as recent activity, vitals, and sleep, and will adjust as the day continues.
