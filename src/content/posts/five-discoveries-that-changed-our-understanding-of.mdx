## Why This Matters

Rex has since transformed into a predator different from most other giant, carnivorous dinosaurs. Rex was originally envisioned as a slow-moving, tail-dragging terror of ancient swamps, facing off against Triceratops in epic, blood-spattered battles. Rex we know and love is understood as a hot-blooded, possibly feathery carnivore that played a critical role in its ancient ecosystem, feeding on naive and injured herbivores while also cleaning up the carcasses of adult Triceratops and Edmontosaurus that were too large to hunt.
