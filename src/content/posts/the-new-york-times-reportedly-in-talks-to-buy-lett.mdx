## What's Happening

Real talk: Letterboxd, an online platform for movie lovers, has grown dramatically in recent years.

The New York Times company has reportedly entered exclusive negotiations to acquire Letterboxd for $330 million, according to a report from The New York Times itself. Letterboxd, an online platform for movie lovers that could be compared to Goodreads for movies, has grown dramatically in recent years. (shocking, we know)

Letterboxd reported 3 million users in January 2021, a figure that grew to 28 million this year.

## Why This Matters
