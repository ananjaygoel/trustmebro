## The Details

The statement, under the title Pacing the Frontier , turns on one sentence: “We request that the U. Government support an international effort to develop the technical and governance tools needed to deliberately pace the frontier of automated AI development.

” The page listed 1,132 signatures and remains open to verified employees of frontier AI companies. Jakub Pachocki, OpenAI’s chief scientist, and Mark Chen, its chief research officer, signed alongside Jared Kaplan, Anthropic’s co-founder and chief science officer, and Chris Olah, another Anthropic co-founder.

## Why This Matters

Shengjia Zhao, Meta’s chief scientist, and Anca Dragan, who runs AI safety and alignment at Google, are on the list, as is John Schulman, chief scientist at Thinking Machines. What the signatories are asking for The statement’s argument is that the leading labs believe they could be close to automating AI research itself, and that industry, government and society may need the option to buy time before that happens. No single company or country can create that option alone, it says: each is under competitive pressure not to slow down unilaterally, and the world rn has no technical or governance machinery capable of pacing frontier-wide progress.
