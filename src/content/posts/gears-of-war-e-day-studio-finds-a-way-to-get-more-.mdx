## The Details

News by Sherif Saed Senior Contributor Oct. Follow Gears of War: E-Day Prioritise us on Google As a first-party Xbox title, Gears of War: E-Day is under pressure to look good and run well on Xbox consoles.

Gears of War: E-Day has densely-detailed environments that require a lot of system memory (RAM), which is a struggle for Xbox Series S' 10GB of GDDR6 RAM. The Coalition's solution was to offload some of that data onto the console's ultra-fast NVMe SSD storage drive.

## Why This Matters

Digital Foundry reported on the tech behind it, which was co-developed by Xbox's Advanced Technology Group. For some context, NVMe SSD drives are made up of RAM modules of their own, so they can act as additional RAM when needed. As fast as they are, but, NVMe drives aren't quite as fast as proper RAM, so The Coalition essentially used the NVMe as a second tier of RAM to help the game's engine stream assets more smoothly.

Gaming fans have strong opinions about moves like this, and for good reason.
