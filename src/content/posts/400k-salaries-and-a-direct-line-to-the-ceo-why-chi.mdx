## What's Happening

So basically About 75% of chiefs of staff are promoted after leaving the role.

Want to learn how to run a company? Sitting a few feet from the person who already does it may be one of the most valuable career moves available. (we're not making this up)

Recommended Video Chief of staff roles are booming across corporate America, offering ambitious workers a rare combination of executive access, six-figure compensation and a potential springboard into senior leadership.

## The Details

Job postings for corporate chiefs of staff more than doubled year over year, according to Revelio Labs data reported by Bloomberg , while the number of people holding the title in North America has more than tripled since 2021, according to Live Data Technologies. The compensation can match the access.

Senior chiefs of staff can reportedly command salaries above $200,000, while some positions in technology and finance can reach $400,000 or more, plus equity. But the biggest perk may be what happens inside the job.

## Why This Matters

A chief of staff can spend every day alongside a CEO or another senior executive, helping decide which problems deserve attention, translating strategy into action and coordinating projects across departments. They may help prepare board materials, sit in on critical meetings, track company priorities, troubleshoot stalled initiatives, and act as a sounding board for the executive they support. That proximity provides something difficult to replicate through a traditional corporate career path: a panoramic view of how a company operates.

Market watchers are paying close attention to developments like this.

## Key Takeaways

- Chiefs of staff help executives manage competing priorities and make sure their decisions translate into action across the company.
- The role can also accelerate the career of the person doing it.
- McKinsey analyzed publicly available data on roughly 250 chiefs of staff and found that the median tenure in the role was just 2.
