## The Details

After CAR T cell therapy, seven women had healthy newborns and five more were experiencing regular pregnancies as of late May, researchers report July 29 in the New England Journal of Medicine . The study is the largest snapshot to date of pregnancy after using CAR T cell therapy for autoimmune diseases, in which the immune system fires at the body’s own tissue.

Untreated, these diseases pose risks to pregnancies , including miscarriage, preterm birth and low birth weight. For our We summarize the week's scientific breakthroughs every Thursday.

## Why This Matters

CAR T cell therapy was pioneered as a treatment for cancer , but there are few reports of pregnancy in those circumstances. Often, those patients are no longer in their reproductive years or are taking other toxic drugs. More just, the therapy has shown effectiveness against autoimmune diseases.

Scientists and researchers are watching this development closely.

## The Bottom Line

More just, the therapy has shown effectiveness against autoimmune diseases. Immunologist Georg Schett, of Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg in Germany, and his team reported in 2022 on five patients who received the therapy for lupus and went into remission .
