## What's Happening

So basically A Meta spokesperson insisted that the updated terms did not reflect a change in the way Meta crawls the web.

Meta just updated its commercial terms of use, declaring that the company can “use any content from [a business account’s] domains, web pages, or URLs to provide, develop, improve, research, train, and maintain the integrity of” Meta products and services. (let that sink in)

According to legal the experts interviewed , this looked like a “remarkable provision” that could give Meta permission to scrape the site of any publisher with a Meta business account and use the content to train the company’s AI models.

Tech companies have been making moves like this as competition heats up.
