## The Details

Droughts, intensive downpours and diseases have created challenges for tea growers across the world as they deal with the impact of a warming planet. But some the experts believe Scotlands relatively settled climate could give the tea industry here a helping hand.

Image caption, Only the top few leaves are picked from each bush to make the immaculate tea As the picking season draws to a close, Susie plucks the last few leaves before proudly pouring me a small glass of her Kinnettles Gold, which is grown in a walled garden on the family farm near Forfar. She believes Scotlands latitude – sitting 57 degrees north - will always be a bar to having a year-round crop like those countries close to the equator.
