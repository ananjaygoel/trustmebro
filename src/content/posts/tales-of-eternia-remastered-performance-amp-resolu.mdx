## What's Happening

Breaking it down: Bandai Namco's role-playing title Tales of Eternia Remastered is on its way to the Switch and Switch 2 next month.

## The Details

Firstly, the frame rate for these versions of the game will run at "up to 60fps". Bandai Namco notes how "the frame rate may fluctuate temporarily depending on gameplay conditions and environment".

As for the resolution, the Switch 2 version runs at 4K in docked mode, and 1080p in handheld mode. And on the original Switch system, it will hit 1080p in docked and 720p in portable play.
