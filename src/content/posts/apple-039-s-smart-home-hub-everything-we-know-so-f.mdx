## The Details

It is expected to be square rather than rectangular, and it will measure in at 6 inches with quarter-inch black bezels around the edges. For size, it will look similar to two iPhones that are side-by-side, and it will be about as thick as an iPhone.

Apple imagines people having multiples of the hub in different rooms of the house. The device will support touch input for opening apps and controlling connected devices.

## Why This Matters

Apple is making two versions of the home hub. One will have a 6-inch display and is meant to be mounted on the wall, and the second version has the same 6-inch display and a hemispherical speaker base that looks similar to the iMac G4. The display attaches to the base with a polished metal arm that holds the small screen and allows it to be tilted forward or backward.
