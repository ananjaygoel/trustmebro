## What's Happening

Let's talk about Werwulf director Robert Eggers dropped hes "not eager to continue purposely doing Robert Eggers House Of Horror" and thinks he needs a break from the genre.

In a recent conversation in Mexico City ahead of Werwulf s December release, the writer-director discussed his approach to making movies that are dark, a little (or a lot) weird, and, at times, alienating ( via Deadline ).

## The Details

Eggers dropped that 2024s Nosferatu was the pinnacle of planning and executing his vision and was meticulously put together. In contrast, his latest film, Werwulf , made it impossible to achieve everything planned because it was a difficult shoot.

Eggers dropped that the crew having to think on their feet actually worked out for the best and made the movie what he always imagined. But hes good on horror for a while.

## Why This Matters

Im proud of that, he dropped of Werwulf . But Im also not eager to continue purposely doing Robert Eggers House Of Horror. I dont need to do The Mummy and Phantom Of The Opera .
