## Key Takeaways

- For those who can't go a few minutes without checking their cell screen, simply setting a limit on apps usually doesn't help much.
- With a dumb phone, you can't even install most of those apps.
- Many dumb phones are limited to running built-in apps for making calls, sending messages and setting alarms.
