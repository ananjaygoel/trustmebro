## Why This Matters

According to government and private sector estimates quoted House, between $30bn (£22. 2bn) and roughly $300bn in goods have been moved from countries with higher tariff through those with lower rates. The process is known as transshipping, which refers to the practice of transferring cargo through another country while en route to a final destination.
