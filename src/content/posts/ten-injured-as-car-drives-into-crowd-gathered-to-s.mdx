## What's Happening

Real talk: Newcastle Knights CEO Peter Parr has expressed his deep concern after a vehicle drove into a crowd of people supporting the NRL team on Saturday, as they prepared for their Grand Final against Sydney Roosters.

Police say 10 people were injured in the incident in Wallsend, New South Wales, including several children, while the 18-year-old man driving the vehicle has been arrested. Thousands had been cheering the team on this week as they trained ahead of Sunday's Grand Final at Accor Stadium, Australian Associated Press reported. (yes, really)

Two of the injured, a five-year-old girl and a 67-year-old man, are in critical condition, and the other eight are in stable condition at a hospital, according to New South Wales Police.
