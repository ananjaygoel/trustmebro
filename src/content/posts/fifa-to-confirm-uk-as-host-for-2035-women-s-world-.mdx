A delegation will be present at Fifas Zurich headquarters with national associations holding a specially arranged virtual meeting, and voting to ratify the co-hosts.

## The Details

The tournament would involve 104 matches contested by 48 teams over 39 days. Nineteen venues are proposed by the All Together bid, with 14 in England, one in Northern Ireland, two in Scotland and two in Wales.

Fifas evaluation of the bid, published earlier this month, rated it 4. But its contractual framework was rated medium risk, and the document claimed that "certain government guarantees contain material qualifications, particularly regarding tax and safety and security".
