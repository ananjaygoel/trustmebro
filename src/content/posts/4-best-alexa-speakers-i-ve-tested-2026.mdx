## The Details

The most recent were shipped late last year: the Echo Dot Max, the Echo Studio (2nd Gen), the Echo Show 11, and the Echo Show 8 (4th Gen). But Amazon has plenty of older, cheaper models available, too.

If youre looking for something small and simple to control your home, either an Echo Dot ($80) or the Echo Dot Max ($120) is your best bet. The Dot Max has fr wild sound for its compact size and is my overall favorite Alexa speaker, but if youre only going to use it for voice commands rather than jam sessions, you can get away with the older, cheaper model, especially if you want to buy a few to place around your home.

## Why This Matters

For anyone looking for a smart display, the Echo Show 11 ($250) is the best of Amazons newest smart displays that come designed for the updated assistant, Alexa+. The older third-generation Echo Show 8 ($150) is still a solid value if you want something cheaper.
