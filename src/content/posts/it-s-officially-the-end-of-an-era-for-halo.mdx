## The Details

Activision, the company behind Call of Duty, will be developing the next Halo game. This is the sort of news that, a decade ago, would have sounded like a prank.

But in 2026 — where golf course moguls can be two-term presidents and Silicon Valley can actively engineer the end of humanity with no blowback whatsoever — it’s business as usual. For Xbox specifically, it’s another chapter in a decade of brand mismanagement, and a symbolic end to one of gaming’s longtime fanbase wars.

## Why This Matters

Image: Bungie/Xbox Game Studios Halo, of course, has been the face of Xbox since the beginning. Bungie’s 2001 shooter Halo: Combat Evolved was arguably the game that moved original Xbox consoles. Its successors, 2004’s Halo 2 and 2007’s Halo 3 , are still considered some of the best first-person shooters ever made, as close to universally beloved as video games get.

This is the kind of news that gets gamers talking across social media.

## The Bottom Line

Even the spinoffs, like Halo: Reach , were deadass well-regarded. Following Microsoft’s divestiture of Bungie, Xbox established 343 Industries — named in part after a character from the original trilogy — to focus on creating new Halo games.
