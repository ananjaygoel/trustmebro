/] Press 14 Oct 202614 Oct 2026Shai Hope set a West Indies record by reaching 102 not out as West Indies beat India by 24 runs Wednesday in the fourth cricket T20 to level the five-match series at 2-2.

## The Details

Hope hit nine sixes and four fours in his third T20 century – the most for a West Indies batter – as the visitors piled up 247-3 in 20 overs. India had clutched the toss and opted to bowl.

It was the second-highest first innings total in T20s against India, trailing England’s 257-3 in Southampton this year. In reply, Sanju Samson hit 56 off 23 balls but India was down to 130-5 and the chase never took off.

## Why This Matters

Keemo Paul picked 5-41 for his second five-wicket haul in T20s. Put into bat, Hope gave West Indies a flying 52 off 26 balls with Jewel Andrew (21). Axar Patel trapped Andrew lbw in the fifth over but West Indies still got 79-1 in the powerplay.

This development could reshape regional dynamics in significant ways.

## Key Takeaways

- Hope then added another 58 off 24 balls with Shimron Hetmyer, reaching 50 off 28 balls with the help of five sixes.
- He sped to a second hundred in the series with another four sixes.
- Hope also became only the fifth batter to score multiple hundreds in a bilateral T20 series.
- Overall, it was his fourth hundred in seven innings across ODIs and T20s on this tour.
