## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, The 31-year-old carries knockout power and his size could pose a problem for Alvarez On Saturday, Christian Mbilli will receive the biggest opportunity of his career when he steps in the ring to defend his WBC super middleweight championship against Saul "Canelo" Alvarez.

While Mbilli has yet to taste defeat as a professional, the oddsmakers are favoring Alvarez, who has firmly cemented himself as a first-ballot Hall of Famer. Mbilli was the beneficiary of Alvarez's most recent fight, a loss to Terence Crawford in September 2025. (yes, really)

After Crawford's decision to retire following a career-best victory, Mbilli was elevated from interim to full champion.

## The Details

Mbilli clutched the interim belt two fights ago, knocking out Maciej Sulecki in the first round of their June 2025 meeting. In his final fight before being elevated to full champion, Mbilli battled to a hard-fought split draw against Lester Martinez.

Mbilli is a bruiser with a high-pressure style. He's muscular and strong and that allows him to bully many fighters.

## Why This Matters

Of his 29 career victories, 24 have come via knockout, which speaks to his ability to turn his pressure into finishing power. Mbilli's forward style benefits when he can back opponents toward the ropes. With an opponent trapped, Mbilli will look to throw thudding right hands to the head while also keeping a focus on throwing nasty left hands to the body, which can break opponents down if Mbilli's power doesn't catch them with a knockout shot up top.

This could change the dynamics of the sport going forward.

## Key Takeaways

- It took just 40 seconds for Mbilli to finish Mark Heffron with a body shot in their fight.
- Mbilli also benefits from a willingness to dirty it up in the ring.
- There are few comfortable moments when Mbilli is on his game.
- The downside to Mbilli's style is twofold, and both were on display in his draw with Martinez, a fight where he'd built up a strong early lead.
