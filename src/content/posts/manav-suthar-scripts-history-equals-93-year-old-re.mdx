## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, In a stunning debut, Manav Suthar has made history as the first spinner to secure five wickets in both his first home and away Tests, a remarkable achievement not seen since Mohammad Nisar in 1933.

At just a young age, he also became the youngest Indian to claim ten wickets in a single Test match, eclipsing Harbhajan Singh's previous record. (let that sink in)
