## The Details

Now, scientists have measured its impact. A levitating nanoparticle jiggled when hit atoms or molecules in a nearly empty vacuum chamber, scientists report in the October 23 Physical Review Letters .

The force exerted by a gas is usually the result of many atoms or molecules bouncing around all at once: Think of the air inside a balloon keeping it inflated. But even an individual atom can exert a tiny force.

## Why This Matters

To detect that force, researchers used a laser to levitate a glass sphere , just 100 nanometers across and weighing about a quadrillionth of a gram, inside a vacuum chamber. Then, the researchers added small amounts of a heavy gas, either krypton, xenon or sulfur hexafluoride. The first two are gases made up of atoms, while the third consists of molecules.
