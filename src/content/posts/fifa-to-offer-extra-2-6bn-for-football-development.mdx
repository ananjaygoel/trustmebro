## The Details

The proposal will be made at a meeting of Fifas council on Thursday and comes at a time when the organisation is under pressure following president Gianni Infantinos aborted Fifa Forward Enterprise (FFE) plan. Infantino wanted to sell stakes in the World Cup and other competitions to private investors but faced huge criticism for the proposal, with Uefa, Concacaf and the Asian Football Confederation questioning his position.

Uefa and Concacaf wrote to Infantino in September calling on Fifa to pay at least $10m (£7. 5m) from its cash reserves to each of the 211 member associations.
