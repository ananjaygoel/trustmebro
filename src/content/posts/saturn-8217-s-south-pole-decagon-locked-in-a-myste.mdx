## The Details

An international team led by Agustín Sánchez-Lavega has now locked in the discovery of a Saturn south pole decagon: a 10-sided atmospheric figure swirling over Saturns south pole. NASAs Hubble Space Telescope first captured faint, subtle hints of a 10-vertex polygonal shape in 2023.

Ground-based amateur and professional astronomers noted an undulating band in 2024, which sharpened into a fully defined decagon structure in Hubble images taken in August–September 2025. The discovery team officially published in the journal Science Advances in September 2026.
