## Why This Matters

Because your body isn’t static—and your probiotic shouldn’t be either. In simple terms, probiotics are live microorganisms that help support the balance of bacteria in your gut and other areas of the body. Research continues to evolve, but a comprehensive 2024 review in the Journal of Clinical Medicine highlights that, when used appropriately, probiotics may support areas like gut health, immune function, metabolic health, and overall well-being.
