## Why This Matters

Setting up its own financial services unit would enable Denza to make more competitive financing and leasing offers on models like its €142,000 ($158,810) Z9GT sports wagon. Leasing and financing arrangements account for the majority of premium-car sales in Europe, making competitive monthly payments crucial in winning users.

The business implications here could be significant in the coming months.
