## The Details

Com News Sony PS5 PS Store Industry PS5 Dynamic Pricing Concerns Revived Job Listing company looking for someone to lead initiative by Sammy Barker 2 hours ago : A new PlayStation job listing has sparked a fresh wave of fan concerns regarding dynamic pricing on the PS Store . Spotted by The Game Post , the role – for a Senior Director of Digital Games Monetisation – includes some phrases which have spooked gamers, including a reference to “intelligent pricing”.

According to the listing, candidates will be responsible for maximising “the commercial performance of publisher content”. Responsibilities will include “driving the adoption and evolution of intelligent pricing, expanding the impact of the targeted offers program, and accelerating the development of future monetisation capabilities across the store”.

## Why This Matters

To Push Square on YouTube 172k While the blurb is vague, the fandom are concerned this may signal Sony’s intent to double down on its dynamic pricing experiment from earlier in the year. For those of you who missed the story , the platform holder was caught offering targeted discounts to select users, without clarifying exactly who was eligible for the best promotions and why. It’s important to underline that there was no example of prices increasing ; it was more a case of some users getting deeper discounts than others.

- The theory is that, as Sony learns more about its players, it can use dynamic pricing to trigger certain actions on its storefront.
