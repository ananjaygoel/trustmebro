## The Details

Add narration to Kindle to get audiobooks for free or at a low cost If you like to have the option to either read or listen to your books, you can take advantage of Kindle and Audible crossovers to get the audio version of certain titles for free, or at least less than what you'd pay if you bought them outright on Audible. One option is to add audio upgrades to your purchased Kindle books: you can see which books are eligible and how much the narration add-ons cost using the Matchmaker tool .

If you have a Kindle Unlimited subscription, you can also get lots of Prime Reading books with Audible Narration at no additional cost. Finally, when adding a Kindle book to your cart, look for the Add audiobook checkbox to see how much the companion Audible version costs in some cases, it may be cheaper to purchase the bundle than to use an Audible credit or buy them separately.
