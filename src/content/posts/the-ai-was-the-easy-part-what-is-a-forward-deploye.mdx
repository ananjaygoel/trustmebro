In 2012, the Harvard Business Review called the data scientist the sexiest job of the 21st century.

## The Details

Every company suddenly wanted one. More than a decade later, the title everyone is chasing now is the Forward Deployed Engineer .

This engineer is embedded inside a companys operation to make AI models or optimisation algorithms actually work in their messy reality. Unfortunately, few operations are messier than a supply chain.

## Why This Matters

I learned it the hard way when I became Supply Chain Data Scientist. For example, imagine a shipment of luxury bags leaving a warehouse in Milan, passing through trucks, airports, and customs clearance before arriving at a store in Shanghai. Distribution Chain of the Fashion Retail company (Image ) This chain involves several teams in multiple countries that use different systems (ERP, WMS, TMS) that were not designed to provide harmonised data.

This adds to the ongoing AI race that's captivating the tech world.
