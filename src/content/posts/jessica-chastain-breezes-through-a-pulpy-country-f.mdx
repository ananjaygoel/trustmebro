One of country musics rising stars, Charlie Heart (Carter Faith), has gone missing.

## The Details

While the police and her handlers chalk it up to a pop divas rough behavior, her father turns to his sister, Charlies aunt and fellow country singer Misty Jones (Jessica Chastain), to see what she can kick up down in Nashville. Reluctantly, she returns to the town that broke her heart to look for her niece, taking matters into her own hands when no one else comes looking for Charlie.

The premise of Heartland , the latest film from Country Strong s Shana Feste, is admittedly silly, but the film leans into its pulpy crime drama roots, its danger, and the mystery these characters find themselves in. The capital of country music is given a Taken -like makeover in Heartland , where shady men prey on rising starlets, no part of the business is safe from corruption, and its tough-as-nails heroine becomes a Liam Neeson-esque figure on a rescue mission.

## Why This Matters

Although theres nowhere near enough fight scenes to make this an action movie, the mystery of Charlies disappearance still comes with a few fisticuffs, bullets, and acts of vandalism. Feste and cinematographer Khalid Mohtaseb give the bright lights of the clubs and festival stages a neo-noir sheen, in which characters exchange tough talk on the way to a performance or talk show, where they switch from threats to smiles for the cameras. Like a Pam Grier-coded heroine in cowboy boots, Misty becomes an independent investigator, pursuing her own brand of vigilante justice when the cops continue to fall short of their duties.
