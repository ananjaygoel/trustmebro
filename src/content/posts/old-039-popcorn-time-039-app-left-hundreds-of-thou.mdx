## What's Happening

Breaking it down: If you installed Popcorn Time on a Mac years ago, part of the app may still be running, and it may have left your Mac open to attack.

Security firm Eye Research has detailed how Time4Popcorn – an unofficial version of the once-popular movie streaming app – left behind a small update checker that scanned for updates every time the Mac kicked off, even for people who hadn't opened the app in years.
