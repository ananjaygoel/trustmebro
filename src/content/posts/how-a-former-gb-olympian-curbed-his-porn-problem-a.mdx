## Why This Matters

So how can you spot when a porn habit has become unhealthy, and how can you tackle it? Although it has become a common phrase, "porn addiction" is not an officially recognised condition in its own right. "We know a lot of people have a problem with porn," explains sex and relationship psychotherapist Silva Neves, who says the matter has become more prevalent among clients at his private practice.
