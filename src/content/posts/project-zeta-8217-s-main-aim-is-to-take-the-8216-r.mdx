## What's Happening

So get this: Hands-on: This South Korean online battle game makes it impossible for the player to avoid combat Feature Project ZETAs main aim is to take the running away part out of Capture the Flag Hands-on: This South Korean online battle game makes it impossible for the player to avoid combat Words by Chris Scullion 6th Sep 2026 / 1:10 pm For a match type that features in a lot of online shooters, a fairly large part of Capture the Flag is about avoiding conflict.

Sure, it takes courage to infiltrate your opponent’s area and steal their flag, but after that it becomes a matter of – to put it bluntly – running away as fast as possible. It’s this part of the game that South Korean developer Nirvanana hopes to tackle with Project ZETA, its ‘multi-team tactical arena’ game which has been running a community beta this weekend. (plot twist fr)

I spoke to some of the Nirvanana team, including CEO Namseok Kim, at Gamescom ahead of the beta’s launch, to find out more about their plans for Project ZETA, which is coming to PC and PS5 at some point in the future.

## The Details

For now at least – and at launch, whenever that ends up being – the game will revolve around a single 12-player gameplay mode, featuring four teams of three players. At its core, the aim is to collect Prisms, take them to a large hole in the ground, wait there for a set time to secure the area then deposit the Prism in the hole, scoring a point.

The first team to score five points, or the team with the most points after the 24-minute timer ends, wins the game. What makes Project ZETA somewhat different from similar games is that Nirvanana has put numerous measures in place to ensure the player is frequently put into battle situations.

## Why This Matters

Often an ideal scenario in a Capture the Flag game is sneaking in and sneaking out again without engaging in conflict, but that can’t happen here. Project Zeta gameplay For starters, the large map is filled with clusters of enemies, who can be demolished to level up your abilities and earn currency which can be spent on perks in the shop.
