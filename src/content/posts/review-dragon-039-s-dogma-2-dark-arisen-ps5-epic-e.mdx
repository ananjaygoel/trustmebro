## The Details

Much like the first Dark Arisen, the new Dark Arisen revitalises the entire experience. It's both an addition to the existing campaign, and an adventure that stands tall on its own terms.

Indeed, this is a big DLC — much larger and more in-depth than I had anticipated going in. It took me around 50 hours to get through its main story and numerous side quests, before being greeted with a full-on endgame.

## Why This Matters

In some ways, Dark Arisen feels like a sort-of sequel rather than a simple add-on. You don't need to have beaten Dragon's Dogma 2 to access it, but I'd highly recommend doing so, because the expansion deserves to be set aside as its own excursion. It takes place in a brand new region to the north; another open world map that's surprisingly vast and impressively dense — and it's this density that makes it a joy to explore, in all of its wintery glory.

Gaming fans have strong opinions about moves like this, and for good reason.

## The Bottom Line

Your journey begins on grassy plains, but as you venture farther into Norgan, you're greeted with frigid mountain passes and blizzard-battered snowfields. Freezing cold temperatures and blinding weather effects play a part, as you'll need to don thermal armours and make use of items like Heatstones to stave off the stamina-sapping cold.
