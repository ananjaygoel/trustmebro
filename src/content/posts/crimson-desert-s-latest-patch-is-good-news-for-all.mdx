## The Details

1, 2026 Follow Crimson Desert It feels kind of alarming that Crimson Desert is continuing to put out such regular patches months on from its release. Essentially this update is focused on trading, and making doing so a lot easier.

The first point of order in taking this step is the addition of 133 new trading posts, which includes the merchant at the Pailune Trading Post. Some of these apparently open up after making your way through certain quests.

## Why This Matters

You'll also find that the kinds and quantities of goods you can come across at trading posts will be adjusted, and you'll also make more money at posts that are harder to get to by wagon. Besides that, trading too much of one item affects the trading price as well, ya greedy little imp. To help satiate your greed, but, the other notable element of this update is the addition of 24 new types of high-value and four new types of regular goods to be pawned off.

The gaming community has been watching developments like this closely.

## Key Takeaways

- And to make physically shifting your goods easier, you can move them between your wagon and your horse.
- Speaking of the wagon, there's seven new wagon workshops around Pywel that can act as intermediate trading posts.
- Workshops will also let you package and load trade goods now too.
