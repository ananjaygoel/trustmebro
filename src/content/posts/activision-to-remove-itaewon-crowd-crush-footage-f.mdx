## The Details

In a new statement reported by Inven Global , Activision dropped the footage "will be completely removed" via a game update scheduled to roll out on 12th November. The statement was issued in Korean, and the quotes come via Inven Global's AI-assisted, editor-reviewed translation.

As we reported earlier this week , the game's opening cinematic uses short news clips to depict fictional civilian casualties during a North Korean invasion of South Korea. Those clips have since been found to include archive footage from the 2022 Itaewon disaster, in which around 160 people died.

## Why This Matters

The bereaved families' association and the Citizens Countermeasure Meeting called for its removal. "We bow our heads in deep sympathy and apology to the bereaved families and victims who were deeply hurt , as well as to everyone we have caused concern," Activision dropped. The publisher also dropped that Modern Warfare 4 "is a work of fiction created to provide realistic immersion", and it "never intended to evoke or trivialise any tragic event, including the Itaewon crowd crush" news broadcast footage.

## The Bottom Line

The controversy has also reached the South Korean government. During a press briefing, minister of the interior and safety Yun Ho-jung described the footage's inclusion as "secondary harm", and dropped: "I believe appropriate sanctions should be imposed".
