## What's Happening

Breaking it down: Thirteen of the 18 startups in Peak XV’s latest Surge cohort are targeting global markets, while more than half are based in India.

Peak XV Partners, one of the largest venture capital firms investing in markets including India and Southeast Asia with more than $10 billion in assets under management, has increased how much it invests per startup through Surge, its seed-stage investing platform, as it unveils a new cohort of 18 companies. (shocking, we know)

At least three of the companies in this cohort had already raised outside funding, in some cases from Peak XV itself, before joining Surge.
