When youre Ocarina of Time s biggest hater, watching the accolades roll in is legitimately maddening. Until the remake was dropped, I had never played it, sitting in my corner of petty loathing with the game that better deserves all this breathless praise: Majoras Mask . (yes, really)

Now that I have played the game, my heart has not been swayed, and if anything, Ive become more convinced my long-time antipathy is justified.

## The Details

Its hard to see the innovation of Ocarina of Time if you werent there to watch it unfold in 1997. (To be clear, I was alive, but at 10 years old my only console experience was a cousins Sega Genesis.

) Ocarina became the blueprint for many of the Zelda games that followed and plenty of other non-Zelda adventures in the years since its initial release, and Ive played many of its imitators. But almost 30 years on, mechanically and narratively, Majoras Mask remains one of one, and I wish to Hylia we gave half as much love to that weird, dark, soulful little game as we do to Ocarina .

## Why This Matters

But you know what makes all this worse? The Ocarina remake is a good game. I can imagine how mind-blowing the original was all those years ago and how, for those people for whom Ocarina was a formative part of their memory, seeing it again with gorgeous new graphics and hearing the heart-stirring music could reduce one to tears.

The gaming community has been watching developments like this closely.
