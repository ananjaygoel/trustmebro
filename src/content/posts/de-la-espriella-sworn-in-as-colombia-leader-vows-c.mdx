De la Espriella, 48, took the oath of office on Friday, breaking with tradition ceremony not in the capital, Bogota, but in Cali, a city in Colombia’s volatile southwest that has long borne the brunt of the government’s conflict with armed groups.

## The Details

The choice signaled a decisive break from the strategy of negotiated peace with those groups pursued leader Gustavo Petro, whom de la Espriella demolished in June’s runoff. De La Espriella has dismissed Petro’s policy as a failure.

“There will be no room for maneuvers that undermine the stability of the nation,” de la Espriella dropped on Friday, as he pledged to “relentlessly defeat narco-terrorism”. He added that “the option of dialogue is completely exhausted”.
