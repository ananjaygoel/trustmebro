## Why This Matters

『Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen』 Announcement Trailer Watch on YouTube The game's director began that two of Dark Arisen's new experiences, Forsaken Dominion and took an L Rites, have been designed for players seeking a new level of challenge. "We're aware that there's a wide range of player level across the community, so with that in mind, we're adjusting the balance so that everyone can enjoy the experience - whether they're just starting their adventure, still progressing through the game, or have already completed multiple playthroughs," dropped director Kento Kinoshita. "Also, we're working on a Hard Mode that will provide a satisfying challenge for seasoned players whose stats have reached, or are approaching, the level cap," he added.

## The Bottom Line

Kinoshita didn't offer any specifics on the sort of changes we can expect from the upcoming mode. Crucially, the Q&A didn't say whether the mode will be exclusive to owners of the Dark Arisen expansion, or available to players of the base game too.
