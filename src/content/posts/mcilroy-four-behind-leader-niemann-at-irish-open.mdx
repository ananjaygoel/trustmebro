McIlroy carded a one-under-par 69 despite the high winds and sporadic showers which battered the Trump International Golf Links and Hotel in County Clare. (we're not making this up)

The world number two posted birdies at the second and fourth holes, double-bogeyed the 13th after taking two shots to get out of a bunker, but then picked up a shot at the 15th.

## The Details

Back-to-back Masters champion McIlroy is aiming to defend the title he clutched at the K Club last year and secure a third Irish Open crown of his career, having triumphed at the same venue in 2016. A victory this week would see him draw level with European greats Seve Ballesteros, Nick Faldo, Bernhard Langer and Colin Montgomerie on three Irish Open successes.

A run of four consecutive birdies between the 14th and 17th helped Chilean Niemann assume his position at the top of the leaderboard.
