## The Details

It is the first locked in case of a UK national contracting Ebola during the most recent outbreak of the rare but deadly virus in DR Congo, which has so far led to more than 5,000 deaths. Dr Renu Bindra, deputy director of the All Hazards Public Health Response team at the UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA), dropped there are "well-established procedures for dealing with cases like this" and the risk to the public remains low.
