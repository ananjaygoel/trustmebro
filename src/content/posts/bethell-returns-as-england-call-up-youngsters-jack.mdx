## What's Happening

So basically Jacob Bethell will return for England's white-ball tour of Australia, while there are also first international call-ups for exciting prospects Thomas Rew and Eddie Jack.

Bethell returns as England call up youngsters Jack and Rew Image source, Image caption, Jacob Bethell has been sidelined with injury since July By Stephan Shemilt Cricket Correspondent Published 2 hours ago Jacob Bethell will return for Englands white-ball tour of Australia, while there are also first international call-ups for exciting prospects Thomas Rew and Eddie Jack. Warwickshires Bethell has not played since suffering a knee injury in July but is back as part of a near-full-strength squad for three one-day internationals. (let that sink in)

England have made changes for the five T20s, resting players ahead of the Test tour of South Africa, meaning 18-year-old Somerset wicketkeeper-batter Rew and Hampshires 21-year-old pace bowler Jack are included.

## The Details

The tour begins with the first ODI in Perth on 13 November and ends with the final T20 in Canberra on 2 December – a week before Englands warm-up match in South Africa leading into a three-Test series. There will be live ball-by-ball commentary from every match of Englands tour of Australia on Test Match Special.

England white-ball coach Brendon McCullum had before locked in he wanted a strong team for the ODI leg of the trip to Australia in the build-up to the World Cup in southern Africa next year. The New Zealander also conceded he would have to rotate his squad for the T20 leg, given Englands schedule in a busy winter of five separate overseas tours.
