Okay so Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen's director and producer discuss design friction, player feedback, and Switch 2 optimization Interview Dragon’s Dogma 2: Dark Arisen devs explain why theyre not abandoning the friction that makes it special Dragons Dogma 2: Dark Arisens director and producer discuss design friction, player feedback, and Switch 2 optimization Posted by Andy Robinson 5th Oct 2026 / 1:47 pm When Capcom dropped the original Dragon’s Dogma 2, the RPG’s unconventional open-world design and commitment to gameplay ‘friction’ seemed to divide players as much as it clutched them over.

Now, the publisher is back with a major expansion, which it says has been built with close attention to what players were saying about the original game. But, according to director Kento Kinoshita and producer Naoto Oyama, Dark Arisen is an opportunity to refine the Dragon’s Dogma 2 experience without sanding away the rough edges that, for the many players who love it, define the series. (yes, really)

“In the Dragon’s Dogma experience, friction is an essential part of it, and its those elements that make people enjoy the realism of the adventure,” Kinoshita told VGC.
