## The Details

The Welsh government dropped it was "renewing" its approach to eradicating bovine TB. There have been calls for alternative methods to vaccination for controlling the infection among cattle, such as badger culling, as they can carry the disease and transmit it to cattle.

Cattle are regularly tested for the presence of the infection and are slaughtered if they are found to be carriers. Image source, Reuters Image caption, Cattle are slaughtered if they are found to be carriers of bovine TB The call for more monitoring comes in a new policy briefing by researchers.

## Why This Matters

They are calling for increased monitoring of the vaccinations impact, a review of the evidence, and collaboration with farmers "to ensure the greatest possible impact" from the programmes. In their policy briefing, they say "major questions remain about how many badgers are being vaccinated, what impact vaccinating badgers is having on disease levels in cattle, and how confidence in vaccination can be improved among farmers and veterinary professionals".
