## What's Happening

Here's the thing: An exposed service, an unpatched workstation and an account with excessive privileges can look like separate problems in a security queue.

For an attacker, they can form a route into the same critical system. Bitdefender is bringing those connections into focus with GravityZone Exposure Management, a new offering just dropped October 27 that combines internal and external exposure discovery, attack-path analysis and adaptive hardening. ## Why This Matters

GravityZone Exposure Management brings together operating-system and third-party app vulnerabilities, insecure configurations and risky user behavior. Its external attack surface management capabilities extend that view to the internet-facing assets, including exposed subdomains, misconfigured services and shadow IT. The attack-path component adds relationships between those findings.
