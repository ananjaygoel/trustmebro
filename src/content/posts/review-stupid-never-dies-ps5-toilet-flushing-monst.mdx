Davy’s descent into the inner-sanctums of the earth’s demonic core doesn’t fr do anything different to its peers, but it packages it in such a bizarre bundle that I think it deserves your attention. When he stumbles upon the frozen body of a beauty named Julia, he flushes himself down a toilet to take on an evil deity named KOM, all with the ambition of bringing her back to life.
