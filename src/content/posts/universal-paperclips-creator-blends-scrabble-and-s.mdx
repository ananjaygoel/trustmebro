If you fantasize about twisting your affinity for words into more traditional combat, the creator of Universal Paperclips has just the game for you. (and honestly, same)

Blending together the fundamentals of Scrabble , tower defense strategy games and some roguelike seasoning, Towers of Babble is immaculate for the logophile looking for a more bespoke Scribblenauts .

## The Details

Each campaign in Towers of Babble begins with a handful of letter tiles. Spell a noun and you can build a unit on the map.

Like larvae in an anthill, words are predeterminedly assigned to harvesters or defensive units. Spell out verbs and modifiers to create power-ups for your nouns.

## Why This Matters

Use your harvested resources to purchase upgrades, such as a larger selection of tiles or adding premium stats to certain letters. Survive enough waves of enemies and you’ll have conquered the level, itself assigned to a new word, and can carry over certain elements to your next struggle session. Created dev Frank Lantz, best known for the absurdist industrial simulator Universal Paperclips , Towers of Babble boasts 120,000 levels and some 200,000 word units to choose from.
