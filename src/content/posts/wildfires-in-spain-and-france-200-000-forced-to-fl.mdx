Aggressive wildfires raging in Spain and France have caused some 200,000 people to evacuate, some fleeing by boat.

## The Details

Extreme temperatures topping 102°F (39°C), combined with hot winds, have fueled the intense wildfires across central Spain (near Madrid and Ávila) and southwestern France (in the Gironde and Landes departments around Bordeaux), leading to the massive evacuations and multiple regional emergency declarations. As of this morning, the fires had burned record acreage, but destroyed only about 50 homes in France so far (no numbers are available yet for Spain).

Both residents and holidaymakers have evacuated. Some of the tourists had gathered prior to the August 12 solar eclipse in Spain.
