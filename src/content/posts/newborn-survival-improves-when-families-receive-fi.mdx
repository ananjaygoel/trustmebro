## Why This Matters

The citys neonatal mortality rate dropped from 10 deaths per 1,000 live births to 5 deaths per 1,000 live births , researchers report November 2 in JAMA Pediatrics . The early neonatal mortality rate, covering deaths in the first week of life, fell from 8 per 1,000 to 2. For the rest of Michigan, rates were stable over the study’s time period.

This could have implications for future research in this area.
