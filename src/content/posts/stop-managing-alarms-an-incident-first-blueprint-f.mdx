## The Details

It is treating every symptom of an outage as a separate problem. It can produce a flood of downstream alarms across routers, transport links, base stations, probes, service KPIs, and customer-care channels.

A conventional NOC sees hundreds of red tiles. An effective operations system sees one evolving incident, estimates its customer and SLA impact, identifies the most plausible upstream cause, and either executes a proven low-risk repair or gets the right human on the case ASAP.

## Why This Matters

That is the practical shift behind modern telecom AIOps: from alarm-centric operations to incident-centric service assurance . It is especially consequential for operators serving tens of millions of rs, where alert fatigue quickly becomes a quality-of-service and leadership problem.
