Com account Thirteen years ago, when Grand Theft Auto 5 was originally dropped, the fandom were confused as to why Rockstar had downgraded its signature series Wanted system . (yes, really)

The mechanic, which informs players of how ardently the in-game police will pursue them, went from six stars in Grand Theft Auto 4 to a mere five stars.

## The Details

Recent gameplay leaks of the open-world shooter reveal that the classic Wanted system is back. Grand Theft Auto 6 ' s cops may have even been upgraded.

Clips that display the Wanted system in action keep getting pulled down by GTA 6 publisher Take-Two, but Polygon reviewed the footage closely prior to the DMCA claim. In it, game co-protagonist Jason can be seen exiting his car to beat a man with a wrench.

## Why This Matters

Curiously, you can hear police over the megaphone before Jason gains any stars. As sirens wail in the distance, police communicate with one another about Jason's whereabouts via megaphone. It almost seems the mechanic is built to function as a warning so that players can change course.

Gaming fans have strong opinions about moves like this, and for good reason.
