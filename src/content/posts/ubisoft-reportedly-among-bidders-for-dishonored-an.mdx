## The Details

Recommended Videos Bloomberg’s Jason Schreier says Ubisoft is among “multiple” business entities that have made offers to Microsoft, along with “at least two other gaming companies,” according to anonymous sources familiar with negotiations. The French connection is there for sure, with the Lyon, France-based Arkane garnering interest from the famously French publisher Ubisoft, so it may be a match made in baguette heaven.

A recent report stated that Arkane had just a few weeks to find a potential buyer or risk full closure, meaning hundreds of jobs would be at risk along with the future of the Blade game that’s been in the works for at least a few years. Earlier this year, Xbox just dropped it was looking to divest Arkane as part of CEO Asha Sharma’s “reset” of the company in efforts to make it profitable.
