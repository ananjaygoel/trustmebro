## What's Happening

Listen up: "With launch still months away, a large public build felt like too big a risk" ReVamp developer cancels public playtest in case AI users reverse engineer it and publish a clone first With launch still months away, a large public build felt like too big a risk Video Game News 20th Oct 2026 / 11:16 am Posted by Chris Scullion An indie studio has cancelled its plans for a public playtest of its next game because its worried it could be stolen by AI.

Digital Sun – which is based in Valencia, Spain and has dropped such titles as Moonlighter, Cataclismo and The Mageseeker – had planned to hold a public playtest of its next game ReVamp on Steam . Its now posted an announcement on its socials accounts, but, stating that its public playtest will no longer take place. (wild, right?)

According the studio, the recent slew of people using AI tools to quickly decompile games and and port them to other systems has the studio worried that the same will happen to its game.

## The Details

Its fear is that someone will download the public playtest, use AI to create a clone of it, and start selling that clone before the studio has time to put the finishing touches to its game and release it. So many of you signed up for ReVamps first playtest on Steam (thank you), but weve decided to start with something more contained on our Discord, with the public playtest coming a bit later, it wrote.

We’re keeping ReVamp’s first playtest small for now. Digital Sun - Wishlist ReVamp!

## Why This Matters

Weve been concerned of games being reverse-engineered, cloned and published before the original is even out. With launch still months away, a large public build of ReVamp felt like too big a risk. To everyone who requested access on Steam – were sorry for the change of plans.

The gaming community has been watching developments like this closely.
