Recommended Videos As expected from a game steeped in The Prince of Tennis lore and geared toward younger players, its story isn’t terribly complex. Seven schools from the series are all taking part in a joint festival over the summer break.

## Why This Matters

You’re on the committee and choose which of the seven schools you work with, getting a chance to romance the tennis player students participating over about a two week period. It’s a fairly traditional visual novel affair, with you selecting where to go each day to interact with someone and choose responses that bolster your relationship and that faction’s chance of having the best booth at the event, thus winning the fair. The Prince of Tennis Sweet School Festival is an otome game banking on quantity in many ways, rather than quality.
