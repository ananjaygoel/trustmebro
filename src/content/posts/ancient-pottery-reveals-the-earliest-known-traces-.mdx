A new genetic analysis of residues left behind on pieces of pottery near the Etowah Indian Mounds has spilled the earliest known traces of cacao east of the Mississippi River, expanding archaeologists’ understanding of the spread of goods and ideas in the ancient Americas.

## The Details

“The Indigenous world of the Southeast was an international world, where people were connected,” says Adam King , an archaeologist at the University of South Carolina. Cacao, which produces beans that are fermented and roasted to create chocolate , originated in Mesoamerica.
