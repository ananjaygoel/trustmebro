## What's Happening

Real talk: For employers filling hundreds of frontline positions, the first interview can become the slowest part of the hiring process.

Dayforce is moving to automate more of that work through an agreement to acquire Ribbon AI, a recruiting technology company whose conversational voice and video AI screens candidates and helps recruiters identify whom to interview next. The just dropped transaction would bring specialized recruiting agents into Dayforces human capital management platform. (plot twist fr)

## Why This Matters

A conversational interviewer that operates around the clock could collect initial evidence of suitability before a recruiter opens the next day’s queue, while leaving the hiring decision with people. From Screening Questions to Adaptive Conversations Ribbon’s interviewer starts with a job description and can generate interview questions and a scoring rubric informed interviews. It conducts a two-way conversation, allowing candidates to answer verbally and the system to ask follow-up questions.
