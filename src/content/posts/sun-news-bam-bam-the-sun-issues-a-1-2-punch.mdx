## What's Happening

Let's talk about Sun news September 5, 2026: Bam bam!

Two eruptions in quick succession sent prominences soaring from both eastern limbs. Their CMEs look set to miss Earth. (yes, really)

The sun issues a 1-2 punch first appeared on EarthSky.

## The Details

Science news, solid photos, sky alerts. Todays top story: The sun surprised us with two blasts in sequence, like a 1-2 punch, with one blast from the suns northeast horizon and one from the southeast.

The first erupted near sunspot region AR4524. It kicked off at around 19 UTC on September 4, and it came with a C6.

## Why This Matters

The second blast, in the southeast, kicked off at around 20:56 UTC on September 4, almost right on the heels of the first. No visible flare accompanied this one, meaning it must have erupted just behind the suns limb, or edge, from an active region whose arrival on the Earth-facing disk is imminent. Both prominences hurled solar plasma into space.

This could have implications for future research in this area.

## Key Takeaways

- But initial analyses suggest the resulting coronal mass ejections (CMEs) are not Earth-directed.
