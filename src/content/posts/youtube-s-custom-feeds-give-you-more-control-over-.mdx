As part of its Made on YouTube event Wednesday, Google dropped several new features, including a Custom Feeds button for adjusting what videos pop up on your front page.

It’s a rare chance for users to directly tweak their algorithmic feed on YouTube, in ways that go beyond just pressing thumbs up on a video or blocking a creator. To try it out, users can click the Your custom feeds button below the search bar and type a request for the feed they’d like to see.
