## Why This Matters

This was Rockstar at a stage of uncertainty. The essence of the Grand Theft Auto franchise was still in flux, and the studio had yet to firmly decide on what, exactly, made a GTA game . GTA Advance has its strong points, but the most important thing about it is how it gave Rockstar (via developer Digital Eclipse) a chance to see where the series could fall short.

The gaming community has been watching developments like this closely.
