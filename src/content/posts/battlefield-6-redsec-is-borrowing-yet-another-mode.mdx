## The Details

Follow Battlefield 6 Prioritise us on Google It's been argued that Battlefield 6 's Redsec battle royale off-shoot is a response to Call of Duty's popular Warzone mode, and today's news is another indication of that. Battlefield Studios has unveiled Rapid Royale, Redsec's version of Warzone's Resurgence.

If you're unfamiliar, Resurgence takes place on dedicated maps that are far smaller than the main mode's large playground, and offers many ways to respawn after death. The pace is majorly faster with more frequent firefights so, and these are the principles guiding the Redsec version, too.

## Why This Matters

Rapid Royale features a reduced player count and is likewise only available on maps smaller than the main mode's. The circles move faster, too, further pushing players closer and closer together. Expect matches that finish quickly, notably a lot shorter than the main mode.

This is the kind of news that gets gamers talking across social media.

## Key Takeaways

- Rapid Royale is one of several new additions coming to Battlefield 6 and Redsec with Season 5, which does not yet have a release date.
- While we wait for that, Season 4's final stage - named Tidal Strike - kicks off 15th September and introduces the first iteration of proximity chat.
- As the name suggests, the feature allows players to communicate with others around them who are not part of their squad.
- It's initially available only in battle royale, with the plan to expand it to Battlefield 6 multiplayer down the road.
