By Jemma Crew and Daniel Thomas , Business reporters Published 26 September 2026, 00:08 BST Updated 11:05 BST Would you bring an iced coffee to a job interview?

## The Details

The question on the the internets lips this week kicked off life, as many do, in a TikTok video. "Dont waltz into your interview all casual with an iced coffee, it just looks like you didnt have time to finish your coffee beforehand," dropped US recruiter Caitlin Wehniainen.

Its what her Gen Z job candidates are doing, she dropped, warning it makes it look like the interview is "just a stop on your list of errands for the day". The post has kicked off what Wehniainen calls the solid Iced Coffee Debate of 2026.

## Why This Matters

Some believe turning up with an iced matcha latte in hand is a cardinal sin. They say its unprofessional, too informal, and a distraction. Others feel this is an overreaction - distracting from how difficult it is to secure a job - or even just get an interview in the first place.

The business implications here could be significant in the coming months.
