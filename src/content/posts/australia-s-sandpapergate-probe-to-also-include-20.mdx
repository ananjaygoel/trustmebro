## What's Happening

So get this: Cricket Australia's fresh independent probe into the sandpapergate scandal has been widened to include the 2017-18 Ashes series against England.

Australias sandpapergate probe to also include 2017-18 Ashes Image source, Image caption, Australia clutched the 2017-18 Ashes series against England 4-0 By Michael Short BBC Sport Senior Journalist Published 27 November 2026, 00:13 GMT Updated 33 minutes ago Cricket Australias fresh independent probe into the sandpapergate scandal has been widened to include the 2017-18 Ashes series against England. The terms of reference for the independent forum have been published and the scope will include "ball-management practices in the Australian mens team during the 2017–18 season", which includes the Ashes series Australia clutched 4-0. (wild, right?)

Also included are all four Tests of the subsequent South Africa tour and the investigation which followed when the ball-tampering scandal emerged.

## The Details

Cricket Australia has pledged to publish the full independent report and recommendations within 30 days of receiving them. All those involved in Australias 2018 tour of South Africa will be invited to speak to the independent forum, though none will have to do so and no retrospective sanctions will be handed out.

The scandal was reignited of former batter Usman Khawajas autobiography. In it, Khawaja claimed the entire team knew about ball-tampering in the third Test of their series against South Africa in Cape Town.

## Why This Matters

The four frontline bowlers in that 2018 team - Josh Hazlewood, Mitchell Starc, Nathan Lyon and current Test captain Pat Cummins - have always shut down knowledge of the plan to use sandpaper on the ball. Cummins has called Khawajas recollection of events "nonsense". But, former team-mates David Warner and Cameron Bancroft - two of three players who were banned for their part in the scandal, alongside Steve Smith - have backed Khawajas version of events.

This could change the dynamics of the sport going forward.
