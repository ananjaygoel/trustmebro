## The Details

Or we simmer in the discomfort because its easier than dealing with the issue at hand. Or, as is the case with Alice Chilton in season four’s fifth episode, the embarrassment is internal, stemming from her feelings and not from someones actions.

Alice gets chagrined at how close Ted has gotten to players shes known longer than him. Theyre interested in his life because hes expressed interest in learning about theirs.

## Why This Matters

Hes an open book, and she remains quite the mystery. Unable to address this situation rationally—something she otherwise seems good at—Alice lashes out at them instead of taking stock of her own attitude. It presents an interesting dilemma for her and Ted, and is the saving grace of an otherwise distressing Ted Lasso hour.
