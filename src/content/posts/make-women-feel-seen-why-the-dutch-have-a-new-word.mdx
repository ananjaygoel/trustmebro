## The Details

Last month, leading Dutch dictionary Dikke van Dale dropped it would include a new term for the labia, "vulvalippen" - in its upcoming online addition - alongside the traditional term "schaamlippen". European campaigners have long been calling for a rewrite of terms used to discuss womens bodies, sexuality and health.

What unites them is the desire to move away from terms that are often seen as misogynistic or judgmental. "Language shapes how we see ourselves," says Shari Klein, one of the founders of the Schaamteloos campaign, which targets words that use "schaam" or "shame".

## Why This Matters
