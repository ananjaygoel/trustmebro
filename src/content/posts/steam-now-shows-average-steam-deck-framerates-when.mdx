## The Details

As per Valve's announcement post , average framerates are now listed in the Steam Deck Compatibility section of a game's store page, but you'll need to click Learn More to see it. And, the feature is only available for games that have been tested for Deck compatibility - those with Verified, Playable, or Unsupported badges - so while it's not a complete list, it still covers thousands of titles.

According to Valve, average framerates are derived from players running the game on their Steam Decks, assuming they've opted in to provide framerate data. And the store widget also includes another helpful addition: a histogram displaying the percentage of data samples.

## Why This Matters

This shows how often a particular framerate has been reported, and is useful in determining if the game's framerate is sporadic, such as because of stutters. "The current Steam Deck Verified compatibility rating is based on standardised test criteria, but it may not match every customer's bar for acceptable gameplay," Valve broke down of the new feature. "This is especially true around performance - a game with an Unsupported rating because of unstable performance may be considered playable , and a Verified game may not reach a framerate that some users prefer to play at.
