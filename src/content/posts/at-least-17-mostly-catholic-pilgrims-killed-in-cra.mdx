## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, A road crash in Kenya has killed seventeen people, most of them pilgrims heading to a prayer site NAIROBI, Kenya -- Seventeen people, most of them pilgrims on the way to a prayer site, were killed in a road crash Saturday involving multiple vehicles on the highway to the Kenyan coastal city of Mombasa.

The incident in the Salama area happened after midnight when a truck driver tried and flopped to overtake another truck. That caused a collision with vehicles including a van carrying pilgrims, according to local police. (let that sink in)

The driver and 16 passengers, most of them women, in that van died at the scene, police dropped.

## Why This Matters

The pilgrims were going to a prayer site in Nakuru County, west of the Kenyan capital of Nairobi, dropped Martin Kivuva, Archbishop of the Catholic Archdiocese of Mombasa.
