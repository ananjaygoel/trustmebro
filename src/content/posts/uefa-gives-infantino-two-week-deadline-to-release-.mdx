## The Details

"Others remain unanswered, and the information and documentation requested have not been provided," it read. The letter included eight pages containing 42 questions covering a number of issues, from the Fifa Forward Enterprise (FFE) private investment, to the Fifa Peace Prize and the controversial suspended ban given to USA striker Folarin Balogun at the World Cup.

"The withdrawal of FFE, the acknowledgement of mistakes and the announcement of potential governance reforms do not answer how the relevant decisions were taken or why the Fifa Council was not involved at the appropriate stage," it continued. "Any refusal to disclose information or documents should be justified individually, with a clear explanation of the specific grounds relied upon.
