As with the Year 1 content, Year 2 will consist of six paid crossover packs, as well as regular monthly free content. The games Year 2 season officially starts on November 3, with the first paid crossover pack based on Godzilla . (and honestly, same)

This will include a new track in which Godzilla attacks the City Escape level from Sonic Adventure 2, as well as three new vehicles based on Godzilla, Mothra and King Ghidorah.

## The Details

Sega had already before locked in that Year 2 would also include a paid DLC pack based on Evangelion , but its now locked in what the other four packs will be. Pack 3 will called Virtual Sanrio Puroland Pack , and will include a track based on the Sanrio Puroland theme park in Japan, as well as Hello Kitty and Kuromi as playable racers.

Pack 4 is the Segaland Pack , and includes a new track based on classic Sega games, as well as three new vehicles based on Segas Out Run, After Butner and Super Megalo arcade cabinets. DLC Pack 5 has a working title of Midgar Pack , and will include crossover content from the Final Fantasy 7 Remake series.

## Why This Matters

The details of this pack have still to be locked in. Similarly, DLC Pack 6 is titled the Reload Revival Royal Pack and will include content from the Persona games, which has also still to be locked in. For free content, the first free DLC character for Year 2 will be added when the season starts on November 3, as Bayonetta arrives.
