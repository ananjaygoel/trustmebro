Com account The 2000s were a solid time for cheesy vampire movies . Shortly before the Twilight franchise turned bloodsuckers into tortured romantic heroes for young adults, countless vampire flicks were dropped, filled with cringy dialogue and shlocky action sequences. (and honestly, same)

Films like Van Helsing and Queen of the Damned aren’t the sharpest of what the genre can offer, but contain insane moments of vampire lore, like an eerie masquerade ball depicting vampires without their reflection, or an undead rockstar climbing the walls to terrorize his groupies.

## The Details

One of the most innovative movies of that era is Daybreakers , which took the cheesy vampire subgenre and transformed it into a dystopian sci-fi thriller. Written and directed Michael and Peter Spierig, this 2010 movie stars Ethan Hawke , Willem Dafoe , and the late Sam Neill , and is streaming for free on Pluto TV.

It’s the rare example of a movie that fully embraces the joys of the vampire genre and is unafraid to be ridiculous or excessive while trying out something completely new. Daybreakers takes place in an alternate and dystopian future of (get ready for it) 2019.

## Why This Matters

Vampires are the dominant species and the remaining enslaved humans are about to go extinct, meaning the main source of food/blood is about to disappear. Hawke plays Edward, a vampire hematologist who doesn’t drink human blood and is developing an alternate food source that will allow vampires to live without harming others.
