## The Details

34 EDT Last modified on Thu 1 Oct 2026 15. 49 EDT Prefer the Guardian on Google Brazil’s attorney general has dropped foreign meddling in his country’s institutions and democracy “cannot be tolerated” after a Guardian report spilled how the Trump administration was pushing to direct funds to a campaign against the Brazilian supreme court.

Writing on X, Jorge Messias called the report “an important alert for Brazilian sovereignty”. “The information is especially serious in light of Brazil’s electoral process and the recent episodes of external pressure on our institutions,” Messias added.

## Why This Matters

Brazil is three days away from its presidential election – the outcome of which Washington has been accused of trying to influence – where the leftwing incumbent, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, will face off against his far-right rival, the Trump ally Flávio Bolsonaro. The pair are neck and neck in the polls. According to the Brazilian television network GloboNews, the attorney general’s office on Thursday filed a criminal complaint to investigate potential wrongdoing, in response to the Guardian story.

International observers are watching how this situation develops.
