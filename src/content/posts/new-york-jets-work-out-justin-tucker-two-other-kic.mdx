## What's Happening

Here's the thing: Tucker, who served a 10-game suspension while a free agent last season, hasn't kicked in the NFL since 2024.

Despite claiming Blake Grupe on waivers Monday, the New York Jets worked out three more kickers on Wednesday, according to a team source, including Justin Tucker. The Jets hosted Tucker, Michael Badgley and Josh Karty. (wild, right?)

No signing is imminent as the Jets intend to give Grupe a real shot at the winning the job for Week 1.

## Why This Matters

The Jets also brought back veteran kicker Jason Sanders, dropped on Sunday, to the practice squad on Tuesday.

This is the kind of move that can define a season.
