The bill passed by 168 votes to 31, with 145 abstentions.

## The Details

It must still go through the Senate before it can become law. So what exactly would the measure do, and why is Morocco watching it closely?

The proposed law would allow people born in Western Sahara before , to apply for Spanish nationality through a special procedure without having to meet the normal residency requirements. First-degree descendants would also be covered.

## Why This Matters

It would also shorten the duration of the residence requirement for eligible Sahrawis legally living in Spain. Instead of the usual 10 years, they would be able to apply after two years. Stans describe the measure as a way of addressing what they see as a historical injustice.

Diplomats and experts are analyzing what this means for international relations.

## The Bottom Line

Spain was the colonial power in Western Sahara until it wrapped up its administration of the territory in 1976. The territory has remained disputed ever since, with Morocco considering it part of its sovereignty, while the separatist Polisario Front seeks independence.
