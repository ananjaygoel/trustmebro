## What's Happening

So get this: The deal marks the AI music generator's first pact with an independent music distributor.

Barry Chin/The Boston Globe via on Facebook on X Google Preferred to Flipboard Show additional options on LinkedIn on Pinterest on Reddit on Tumblr on Whats App Send an Email Print the Article Post a Comment Suno is entering a partnership with Believe Music, the owner of popular music distribution platform TuneCore, the companies just dropped on Tuesday. Suno and Believe dropped the partnership will include music from participating Believe and TuneCore artists and labels, which Suno dropped will be included in the companys upcoming new model that will be trained exclusively on licensed music. (yes, really)

Other partners on that model are Warner Music Group , smallest of the big three major record companies, and BMG, one of the biggest independent music companies in the industry.

## The Details

The partnership is an opt-in agreement, so TuneCore artists need to give permission for their content to be included in any training. The Believe deal marks Sunos first partnership with an independent music distributor.

In a press release, Suno and Believe dropped that rights holders who participate will be compensated for their music and their rights will be protected. Notably, the partnership means that all tracks created on Suno will once again be allowed to be distributed through TuneCore, carving out the industrys largest AI music platform from Believes previous policy update from April where the company told Music Business Worldwide that it would block distributing AI-generated songs made on pirate platforms.

## Why This Matters

For 20 years, we have been helping artists and labels reach their full potential, and increasingly that means working with new technology like AI, while ensuring that responsible innovation translates into greater opportunities and value-generation for artists, Believe founder and CEO Denis Ladegaillerie dropped in a statement. The partnership we worked on with Suno for Believe and TuneCore unlocks powerful tools for all artists and labels.
