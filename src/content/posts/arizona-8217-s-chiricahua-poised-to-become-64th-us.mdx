## The Details

Will it become the 64th national park in the U. A quiet national monument tucked into southeastern Arizona is poised to become North Americas newest national park.

Chiricahua National Monument which towers with rock formations and is home to animals such as the coatimundi could soon become the 64th national park in the United States. Chiricahua National Park would join the three other national parks in Arizona: Petrified Forest, Saguaro and the Grand Canyon.

## Why This Matters

Congress passed legislation to redesignate the monument as a national park. Now the bill just awaits the U. The legislation keeps the existing boundaries of the park.

This could have implications for future research in this area.

## Key Takeaways

- It also doesn’t change the federal ownership and management of the park.
- Chiricahua, sitting in the Chiricahua Mountains, is famous for its bizarre collection of rock formations.
- The National Park Service describes the landscape as a collection of rhyolite pinnacles and balanced rocks.

It’s the home of many astronomy observatories. Chiricahua is open 24 hours a day.
