Some young Yosemite toads emerge from their first winter underground infected with the deadly chytrid fungus Batrachochytrium dendrobatidis , or Bd, researchers report September 23 in Functional Ecology .

Although the fungus typically spreads through water, the finding points to an unexpectedly risky period as toads wait out harsh winters in burrows beneath feet of snow. Bd has hit amphibian populations hard .

## Why This Matters

The fungus attacks amphibians’ skin, preventing electrolytes and fluids from entering the body, and often leads to heart failure. Infections, which can kill the frogs, are responsible for declines in at least 500 species and 90 possible extinctions, according to a 2019 estimate.
