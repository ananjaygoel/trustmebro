## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, You might want to sit down for this.

A ROUND THE WORLD —and on Reddit—there’s an eternal debate raging about whether men should sit or stand to pee. In the US, the answer seems like a no-brainer: standing. (shocking, we know)

One YouGov poll found that only 10 percent of American men regularly sit down to pee, while 31 percent dropped they never do this.

## The Details

(There’s even that viral video years ago of a pastor arguing “a man is someone who pisses against the wall. ”) But that same YouGov poll found that where you wee varies.

In Germany, 40 percent of guys actually sit down every time to urinate. About 22 percent of Swedish men, for example, sit down every time, but 28 percent sit down most of the time.

## Why This Matters

Entire threads online are devoted to this question, including a hybrid tactic as a compromise in which a guy pees sitting down at home but stands up at a urinal in public. Looking beyond preference, is there a right answer to take a leak? One study published in the journal Neurourology and Urodynamics tried to give a definitive answer.

This is the kind of health news that affects everyday decisions.
