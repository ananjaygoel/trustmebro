## What's Happening

Okay so Sea turtles are famous for being prodigious wanderers.

Guided by Earths magnetic field, ocean currents and the sun, they tend to crisscross the seas in search of food, returning to the region of their birth every few years to breed. (yes, really)

So when scientists found out a population of sea turtles that appear to be much homebodies, they were taken aback.
