## Why This Matters

If you bring a grappling hook with you, this will be easier, but there is a ladder on one of its sides that you can use. Once you reach the roof, walk along the side until you find a door. There is just one door, so there should not be any conflict.

- Take the stairs and head down, and you will come across a set of thin windows straight ahead.
- Use your grappling hook to jump to the other side directly.
- You will not be able to reach the other tower and dangle outside the north trench tower.
- If you look down from here, you will find a crate attached to an arm.
