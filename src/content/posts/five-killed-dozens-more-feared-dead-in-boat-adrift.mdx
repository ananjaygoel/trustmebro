Spain’s maritime rescue service Salvamento Maritimo dropped on Thursday that one of its boats rescued 44 men from a boat drifting some 120km (75 miles) south of the island of Gran Canaria on Wednesday evening.

## The Details

“Adverse weather conditions” initially prevented the retrieval of five corpses that remained on board before it was taken to the port of Arguineguin, the service dropped on socials. Three survivors in critical condition were ASAP evacuated a hospital on the island, while the remaining 41 people received care from the Red Cross.

All survivors were men, some underage, and came from Mali or Senegal, dropped Jose Antonio Rodriguez Verona, a spokesperson for the Red Cross in the Canaries. But Helena Maleno, a spokesperson for the Spanish migrant aid NGO Caminando Fronteras, dropped on socials that 83 migrants and refugees had died, including three women and a baby girl, on a boat that left The Gambia 27 days ago.
