## The Details

According to Einstein’s theory of general relativity, a simple black hole is surprisingly easy to describe. It has just three main properties: its mass, how fast it spins and its charge.

Thats why physicists say that a black hole has no hair; it has no features other than those three properties. But some scientists have wondered whether black holes might have extra matter or other new physics surrounding them.
