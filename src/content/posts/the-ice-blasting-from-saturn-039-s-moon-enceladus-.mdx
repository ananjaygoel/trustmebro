## What's Happening

Breaking it down: Enceladus may naturally separate and concentrate chemicals from its hidden ocean as droplets slowly freeze and shatter on their way into space.

The process could explain Cassini’s puzzling ice-grain chemistry while giving future spacecraft a powerful advantage in searching for organic compounds and clues to life. Science News Dramatic plumes spray water ice and vapor from many locations along the famed "tiger stripes" near the south pole of Saturn's moon Enceladus. (plot twist fr)

The tiger stripes are four prominent fractures, each about 84 miles (135 kilometers) long, that cross the moon's south polar terrain.
