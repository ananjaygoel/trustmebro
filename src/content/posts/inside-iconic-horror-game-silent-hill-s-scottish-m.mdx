For the first time, one of gamings biggest horror franchises - created in Japan but traditionally set in the US - has come to the UK, set on a fictional Scottish island.

## The Details

Jon McKellan, director at Glaswegian developers Screen Burn, told me taking on the mega brand was an "absolutely terrifying" prospect for the relatively small team. As a fan of the games, I was keen to find out what one of the most iconic survival horror series looks like in Scotland - and whether it can live up to its legacy.

Set in 1996, the game follows the plight of an American man called Simon Ordell, who washes up on St Amelia with a red hospital ID band around his wrist and an IV drip in his arm.

## Why This Matters

As is to be expected from the Silent Hill games, the real reasons why youre there and whats going on are teased as you progress through the story in a typically vague fashion. For those concerned the game might lean into any typical Scottish stereotypes, McKellan told me he wanted it to feel "authentic and natural".
