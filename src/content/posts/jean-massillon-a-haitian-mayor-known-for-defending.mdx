## What's Happening

So get this: Jean Massillon, the well-known mayor of Kenscoff, a once peaceful farming community in Haiti that has been under assault by violent gangs, has died PORT-AU-PRINCE, Haiti -- Jean Massillon, the well-known mayor of Kenscoff, a once peaceful farming community in Haiti that has been under assault , died on Friday.

Deputy Mayor Jean Max Duvivier locked in the death to radio station Métropole Haiti, with officials saying it was health-related. Massillon was known for fighting for his people and trying to protect them. (we're not making this up)

He repeatedly called on the government to keep them safe and often talked to local radio stations to raise awareness about the struggles of Kenscoffs residents.

## The Details

“Jean Massillon was a man who never shied away from his responsibilities. Even as insecurity weighed on the daily lives of his fellow citizens, he chose to remain present alongside his community and to make his voice heard so that Kenscoff would never be abandoned,” Haitis government dropped in a statement.

Kenscoff was the site of two recent brutal massacres that left at least 164 people dead, with more than 70 kidnapped and dozens missing. A human rights group in Haiti just dropped a report asserting that a “timely strengthening” of security could have prevented or limited the killings.

## Why This Matters

The attacks in July and August displaced roughly 10,000 people.
