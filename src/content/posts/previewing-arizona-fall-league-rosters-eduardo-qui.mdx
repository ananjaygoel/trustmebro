## What's Happening

Breaking it down: The Arizona Fall League rosters are stacked, with several top 100 prospects and many more intriguing players.

This year’s Arizona Fall League season will begin on Oct. 3, and after a day off — I mean, one game is a lot, everyone will need to rest afterwards — the season will continue in earnest for six weeks. (and honestly, same)

As always, there are six teams in the league, all playing in spring training facilities in the greater Phoenix area, with every MLB club sending at least six prospects to the desert for Opening Day.

## Why This Matters

MLB just dropped the initial rosters on Tuesday morning, and they’re loaded, although as usual there’s way more position player talent than pitching — none of the pitchers rn on my top 100 prospect list are going to the AFL.

Sports analysts are already debating what this means for the competition.
