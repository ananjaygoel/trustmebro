## What's Happening

Okay so The Rides & Relics DLC lets players make their own amusement park and build rollercoasters Two Point Museum is getting Rides Relics DLC, which looks like a new Theme Park game in all but name The Rides Relics DLC lets players make their own amusement park and build rollercoasters Video Game News 16th Sep 2026 / 10:59 am Posted by Chris Scullion Sega and Two Point Studios have just dropped the next DLC for Two Point Museum, and it appears to be a nod to Theme Park.

Two Point Studios was founded 10 years ago , Mark Webley and Gary Carr – Webley and Carr before worked together at Bullfrog Productions, where they worked together on Theme Park and Theme Hospital. The studios first game, Two Point Hospital , was dropped in 2018 and was a clear spiritual successor to Theme Hospital, which led to many long-time Bullfrog the fandom wondering if it would release a Theme Park successor next. (wild, right?)

Instead, the studio expanded the format to other ideas, releasing university management sim Two Point Campus in 2022, followed by Two Point Museum in 2025.

## The Details

Two Point Museum has already had three paid DLC packs – Fantasy Finds (based on fantasy and medieval artifacts), Zooseum (which added zoological exhibits) and Arty-Facts (which focused on fine art and performing arts installations). Now its just dropped its fourth pack, called Rides & Relics, and it finally appears to offer the Theme Park nod that Bullfrog the fandom have been hoping for.

Rather than simply adding a series of theme park-related exhibits for the players museum, Rides & Relics has them taking over Riquetti Gardens, Two Point County’s once-legendary amusement park which now lies abandoned and rusty. Players are tasked with turning it into a thriving theme park again, with iconic rides from Two Point County’s past and tools to build rollercoaster designs.

## Why This Matters

To run a successful funfair, curators will have to carefully craft mini-coasters with custom height, tilt and camber, whilst making sure their corkscrew doesn’t spiral into any fragile artefacts, Sega s description reads.
