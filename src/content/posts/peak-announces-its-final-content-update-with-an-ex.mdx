## The Details

Peak Is Getting A Massive New Update This Week Scheduled to launch next week on August 11, the surprise update will introduce not only one, but two new biomes for you to explore either solo or alongside a friend in the form of the Gloom and The Citadel variants. Replacing Caldera and The Kiln in the biome rotation, respectively, the two new maps can only be played back to back as opposed to being randomly connected like traditional biomes.

While we only have the short teaser to go off of for now, the maps seem to consist of a misty open expanse for The Gloom, while offering an entirely vertical tower filled with ladders, traps, and other hazards for the warmer-looking Citadel. Plus to the two maps, Peak also teased the return of a familiar Scoutmaster for you to encounter as well.
