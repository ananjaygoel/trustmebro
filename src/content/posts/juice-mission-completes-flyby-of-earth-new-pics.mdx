## The Details

Science news, solid photos, sky alerts. On Monday, , the European Space Agencys JUICE mission successfully completed a fly.

This was a gravity assist, designed to increase the spacecrafts speed and alter its trajectory as it makes a winding trip to Jupiter. JUICE shipped in 2023 and is because of arrive at Jupiter in 2031.

## Why This Matters

Its job will be to explore Jupiters ocean-bearing moons: Europa, Ganymede and Callisto. During the JUICE flyby, the spacecraft passed just 5,369 miles (8,640 km) above the Indian Ocean. Thats less than 70% of the diameter of Earth itself.

This could have implications for future research in this area.

- Its closest approach was at 6:45 a.

## The Bottom Line

The spacecraft lifted off successfully into cloudy skies, beginning a multi-year mission to Jupiter and its icy moons. As often happens with missions to the outer solar system, the spacecraft has to take a circuitous route to Jupiter, making multiple sweeps past the moon, Earth and Venus.
