It's what we all quote from our favorite movies and, often, the best lines grow beyond the movie that spawned them to have a life of their own within culture. There are solid lines that make us think, funny lines that make us laugh, and warm lines that can make us feel happy or seen.

Then there are the coldest lines that go straight to the bone . What makes any individual line a cold one can come down to a combination of things.

## Why This Matters
