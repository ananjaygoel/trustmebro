## Why This Matters

Talking specifically about the verification process behind claiming a page, which stops short of letting anyone edit their actual credit listings, Denzel dropped: "There's two parts to that. One is making sure that people are who they say they are, and that's a human-driven verification process that we run in the background. When it comes to making sure that credit history is reflected appropriately, the actual way that we do credits and credit verifications has not changed: It's not changed since Atari has been involved, it's not changed in a long time, and we have no plans to change that process".
