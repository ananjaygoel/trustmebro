## What's Happening

Breaking it down: A group of brain cells called orexin neurons appears to help us keep working when reaching a reward becomes increasingly difficult.

Blocking their activity weakened motivation in rats, pointing to a possible new target for understanding why people sometimes struggle to sustain goal-directed behavior. (plot twist fr)
