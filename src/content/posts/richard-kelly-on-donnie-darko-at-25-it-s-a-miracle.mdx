## The Details

It’s a hot Friday in mid-August when I sit down with Kelly, 51, at Santa Monica’s Aero Theatre , a beloved repertory venue that makes a key appearance in Donnie Darko , his remarkable debut feature. Dropped in October 2001 with virtually no marketing because of the tragic events of 9/11 weeks prior (the plot hinges on a plane accident), it couldn’t get a toehold at the box office.

But in the months and years that followed, the film became a hit on the home video market and gained a fervent cult fandom thanks to its unique supernatural mystery. To mark its 25th anniversary, the movie will enjoy another limited theatrical run, beginning on October 2, the date that launches mentally disturbed suburban teenager Donnie Darko (Jake Gyllenhaal) on a surreal journey that may or may not lead to the end of the world.
