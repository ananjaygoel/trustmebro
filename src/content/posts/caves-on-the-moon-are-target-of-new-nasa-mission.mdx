## The Details

We dont know for sure yet, but evidence has been growing that theyre there. Now, NASA has just dropped a new mission to search for moon caves.

Its called GIMLI for Geophysical Instruments for Marius Lunar pit Investigation. The plan is to land the mission at the volcanic Marius Hills Pit in Oceanus Procellarum (Ocean of Storms), a vast, dark lava plain or lunar mare (sea) located on the western edge of the moons near side.
