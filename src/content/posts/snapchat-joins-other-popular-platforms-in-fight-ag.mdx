## The Details

Snap, the parent company of Snapchat, dropped on Friday that the platform would stop recommending "wholly AI-generated videos" in its popular Spotlight feed in favour of "authentic, human-made content. " Over the past two weeks, YouTube, LinkedIn and Substack have unveiled similar strategies.

Snap did not go so far as to try and prohibit all AI-generated content from Snapchat. The platform offers its own AI tools to alter content, and so AI "enhanced or edited" content will still be part of its recommendations to users.
