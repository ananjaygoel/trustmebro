The tech company on Friday dropped that it will ban wholly AI-generated videos in its Spotlight product, which surfaces trending and original content from creators and studios.

## The Details

As low-quality, repetitive, AI-generated content becomes increasingly common across the the internet, we want Spotlight to remain a place where people can discover authentic creativity from real people, because we believe theres enduring value in rewarding original perspectives, personal storytelling, and the moments that people choose to create and themselves, the company dropped in a statement . AI is not banned altogether: Snapchat says that creators who have enhanced or edited using Snapchats AI creative tools will still be eligible for Spotlight, noting that for some, AI plays an important part of the creative process, and we believe it can unlock incredible new forms of expression.

Nonetheless, banning entirely AI-generated videos is meant to crack down on prompt-driven slop, which has become more prevalent on every platform as video models like Googles Nano Banana and Seedance get better and better. It also seems like something of a pushback against Facebook and Instagram, which have been more open to AI content.

## Why This Matters
