## The Details

The Street Fighter movie is getting panned . Not quite as rough as 2024s Borderlands adaptation , but still far from solid.

Starring Noah Centineo as Ken Masters, Andrew Koji as Ryu, and Callina Liang as Chun-Li, Hollywoods latest attempt to cash in on Capcoms longrunning fighting game series marketed itself from the jump as a silly affair. Cody Rhodes looks absurd as Guile.

## Why This Matters

They even let Eric André on screen as ring announcer Don Sauvage. But based on early reviews, unlike Mortal Kombat 2 , which committed to its dumb premise with unflinching B-action movie conviction, Street Fighter sounds like a dizzying mess of abbreviated character arcs and humor that doesnt land. Officially in theaters starting October 16, the live-action adaptation has opened to a 41 percent on Rotten Tomatoes .

Gaming fans have strong opinions about moves like this, and for good reason.
