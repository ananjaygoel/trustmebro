I had Andrej Karpathy’s nanochat, a pair of Nvidia DGX Spark mini supercomputers on my desk, and three ways to run the training math: Transformer Engine’s FP8, its newer four-bit NVFP4, and nanochat’s own FP8 code. Fewer bits and newer tools should mean a faster finish.

## Why This Matters

Only one of the three actually delivered. And the GPU was never fr the problem. One Spark spent a week telling me its GPU was 96% busy while using about a fifth of its rated compute, and adding a second Spark swapped one bottleneck for another, and neither was the one I expected.
