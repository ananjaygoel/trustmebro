## Why This Matters

As production becomes less scarce, a publication’s defensible value shifts toward something AI can imitate sentence cannot establish on its own: a recognizable point of view. Point of view is often mistaken for tone. It is treated as a layer of personality applied after the reporting is finished—a preference for sharp sentences, a degree of formality, or a recurring set of phrases.
