Now, DJI has dropped its follow-up: the Osmo Pocket 4 . Building on the Pocket 3's success, the Pocket 4 makes several changes intended to improve the camera's operation and performance.

## Key Takeaways

- Right out of the box, the most ASAP visible difference is the Pocket 4's redesigned gimbal protection.
- The Pocket 3 had a hard protective cover that cradled almost the entire camera on one side, protecting both the screen and the gimbal.

## The Bottom Line

This clamp slides between the gimbal's moving parts to hold it secure, and easily fits in a jacket pocket when not in use. Paired with a new soft fabric carrying pouch that can fit the entire camera to protect the screen, it's a noticeably more elegant solution.
