## Why This Matters

A high-speed passenger train rear-wrapped up another and caused a derailment on Wednesday morning near Toledo, Spain, killing at least 30 people, according to a spokesperson for the regional government. The fatal accident, which sent multiple carriages down an embankment, comes after another deadly crash involving the same carriers on a southern line in January that killed 46 people, stunned Spain and created deep doubts about the security of its once-vaunted rail system. At least 30 people were killed in the Wednesday crash, according to a spokesperson for Castilla-La Mancha, the region where it took place.
