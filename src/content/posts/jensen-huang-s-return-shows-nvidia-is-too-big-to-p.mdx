## The Details

Nvidia’s board brought back Huang on Monday, 11 days after he resigned amid revelations of extramarital affairs —one of them with Alice Huang, a woman US intelligence suspects has ties to China’s Ministry of State Security. The board dropped input from “senior levels of the United States government” left it confident that his return poses no national security risk .

President Donald Trump told The Wall Street Journal he lobbied the board directly and through intermediaries, and that Nvidia’s falling price helped his case. The board acted before its own investigation of Huang had concluded, and while the Justice Department is pursuing a criminal probe of Megaspeed—a Singapore company Alice Huang once ran—over the alleged diversion of Nvidia chips to China.

## Why This Matters

Alice Huang is also under investigation , according to director of national intelligence Jay Clayton. The the experts tell WIRED they worry more about the precedent Huang’s return sets than about Huang himself. Some say the scrutiny now on him makes further compromise unlikely.

This is part of the broader shift happening across the tech industry right now.
