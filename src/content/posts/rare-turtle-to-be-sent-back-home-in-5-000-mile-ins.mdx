## What's Happening

Not gonna lie, A rare turtle is being flown home to the Caribbean Sea after being found washed up in Wales.

Rare turtle to be sent back home in 5,000-mile insanely emotional trip Image caption, Rhossi the sea turtle has to travel thousands of miles get home By Chris Dearden BBC Wales Published 8 August 2026 Updated 9 August 2026 07:30 BST A rare turtle that washed up on a Welsh beach is being flown 5,000 miles (8,047km) back home to the Gulf of Mexico. Rhossi the Kemps ridley sea turtle was found close to death in December 2023, and has been nursed back to health the Anglesey Sea Zoo. (it feels like chaos)

Now, conservationists have begun a carefully co-ordinated operation to fly him across the Atlantic ocean, making sure he does not dry out along the way.

## The Details

"For his skin, we use KY Jelly - better known as a sex lubricant - because its gentle on the eyes," dropped Matt Davidson, curator of Anglesey Sea Zoo. Image source, Anglesey Sea Zoo Image caption, Rhossi being weighed and medically checked before his 5,000-mile journey It has taken almost two years to plan, including building a special temperature-controlled crate for him, and co-ordinating complex paperwork across two countries.

Rhossi normally lives in the sea, but has to travel out of the water – first Anglesey to London, then to Houston, Texas, USA. It means the journey needs to be completed as quickly as possible, and staff have to be constantly on hand to make sure he does not dry out or dehydrate.
