Breaking it down: US TV news coverage of the White House returns to normal after a judge lifted Trump's ban of three news outlets.

A federal judge had ruled on Thursday that the ban was likely unconstitutional and the administration had to let them back in, at least for now.

## The Details

Trump had barred them for what he called "fake news". After the ruling on Thursday, the White House still shut down entry to some CNN and MS NOW reporters assigned to cover arrivals for a state dinner for Chinese leader Xi Jinping.

But on Friday, White House officials allowed reporters entry and coverage went back to normal for the last day of Xis visit with Trump in Washington. Four television networks that have a daily rotation of White House coverage along with CNN - ABC, CBS, Fox News and NBC - had paused their broadcasts of the president in solidarity with the banned outlets.
